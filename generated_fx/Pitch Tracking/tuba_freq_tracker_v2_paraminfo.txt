================================================================================
00_tuba_freq_tracker — Parameter Information
================================================================================

OVERVIEW
A custom tuba-specialized hybrid pitch & phasor tracker for live low-brass
dubstep, built to avoid the frame latency of generic FFT-based YinFFT
(fluid.pitch~), which needs a 2048/4096-sample window (~46 to 93 ms at 44.1/48 kHz)
and only updates once per hop. It unites three complementary DSP stages inside
a single RNBO codebox~:

1. 4x-Decimated Recursive Sliding YIN (CMNDF): Computes the Cumulative Mean
   Normalized Difference Function in the time domain. The difference function
   is updated recursively over a sliding window (add the newest sample's term,
   subtract the term leaving the window), so nothing is recomputed from scratch.
   The cost is O(maxLag) per decimated sample (up to ~430 lags), not O(1), which
   is still light on CPU. The CMNDF search runs every 32 audio samples (~0.7 ms at
   48 kHz) with parabolic sub-sample interpolation, a first-dip search to reduce
   octave errors, and a 3-point median filter to reject valve/tongue transients.

2. Instantaneous Frequency Tracking (IFT): Isolates the tuba's fundamental with
   a dual-stage adaptive SVF bandpass centered on the fused pitch, builds the
   analytic signal s_a(t) = A(t)*e^(j*theta(t)) with a 4-stage quadrature IIR
   Hilbert pair (z^-2 allpass sections, ~90 degrees +/- 1 degree across 28 to 420 Hz),
   and differentiates the unwrapped phase d(theta)/dt on EVERY sample. There is no
   frame or hop latency, but the narrow bandpass filters still add group delay,
   which grows as pitch gets lower.

3. Confidence-Weighted Fusion + Phase-Locked Loop (PLL): YIN supplies a coarse
   octave anchor, and IFT follows fast lip bends, vibrato, and glissandi within
   +/- 18% of that anchor (it falls back to the anchor otherwise). The two are
   mixed with a fixed crossfade (IFT_speed), and YIN confidence scales how quickly
   the fused pitch adapts. This is a complementary blend, not a Kalman filter: there
   is no covariance or motion model. The last valid pitch is held on note
   releases. A PLL phasor locks to the analytic phase of the fundamental.

   Note: the anchor reduces octave errors but cannot eliminate them. If YIN
   locks to the wrong octave, IFT will follow it.

INLETS & OUTLETS
- in1 / in2: Stereo (or mono on in1) tuba audio input
- in3: Optional external pitch hint in Hz (if fluid.pitch~ is patched here, it is
  averaged 50/50 with the internal YIN anchor; only values between 20 and 1000 Hz
  are used)
- out1: Tracked fundamental pitch f0 in Hz (patch directly into in3 of effects 01–18!)
- out2: Pitch confidence / periodicity [0.0 to 1.0]
- out3: Phase-locked tuba phasor [0.0 to 1.0) (phase-locked sawtooth ramp at f0)
- out4: Phase-locked fundamental sine wave [-1.0 to 1.0] (scaled by confidence)

PARAMETERS (5)

1. min_freq_hz
   - Range: 24.0 to 120.0 Hz (Default: 28.0 Hz, covering down to Pedal Bb0 = 29.14 Hz)
   - Taper: Direct frequency in Hz.
   - Function: Sets the longest lag boundary of the YIN search. Raising this to ~38 Hz
     when not playing pedal notes shortens the lag loop and lowers CPU. It does not
     reduce latency, since the integration window is set separately by yin_window_ms.
   - Note: At sample rates above 48 kHz the lag range is capped at 430 lags, so the
     lowest trackable pitch rises (roughly 55 Hz at 96 kHz) regardless of this setting.

2. max_freq_hz
   - Range: 150.0 to 700.0 Hz (Default: 420.0 Hz, covering up to high G4/Ab4)
   - Taper: Direct frequency in Hz.
   - Function: Sets the shortest lag boundary of the YIN search and the upper clamp
     of the tracker, preventing high brass lip buzz or valve clicks from triggering
     false high-frequency jumps.

3. IFT_speed
   - Range: 0.0 to 1.0
     (0.0 = median-filtered YIN only, 1.0 = full per-sample IFT instantaneous frequency,
     still gated to within +/- 18% of the YIN anchor)
   - Taper: Linear crossfade coupled with tracking time-constant reduction
     (from 10 ms down to 1.5 ms).
   - Function: Controls how strongly the Hilbert phase differentiator (IFT) is
     weighted against the YIN lag estimator during fast lip slurs, pitch bends,
     and vibrato, and how quickly the fused pitch follows the target. The adaptation
     rate is further scaled by YIN confidence (full speed at confidence >= 0.75,
     fading to a hold at confidence <= 0.35).

4. gate_thresh_db
   - Range: -60.0 to -18.0 dB (Default: -42.0 dB)
   - Taper: dB converted to a linear amplitude (dbtoa) and compared against an
     8 ms RMS envelope.
   - Function: Holds the tracked pitch f0 at its last valid frequency whenever the
     tuba's RMS level drops below this threshold, which prevents pitch-drop glitches
     at the end of notes. YIN evaluation is also paused below the threshold. The
     gate has no hysteresis, so keep it clear of your noise floor.

5. yin_window_ms
   - Range: 10.0 to 60.0 ms (Default: 28.0 ms)
   - Taper: Direct time in milliseconds.
   - Function: Length of the YIN integration window. Longer windows give more
     reliable low notes but add latency and smooth the response; shorter windows
     react faster but are less stable. At the default 28 ms, the window is shorter
     than one period of a 28 Hz pedal tone (~36 ms), so the lowest notes are partly
     a guess at onset. Try 36 to 40 ms if pedal tones feel unreliable.
   - Notes: The window is capped so that window + maxLag fits the 1024-sample
     buffer (about 49 ms at 48 kHz with min_freq_hz = 28). Changing this
     parameter while audio runs resets the running sums, so expect a brief
     (about one window) dip in confidence while they refill. Treat it as
     set-and-forget rather than a performance control.

REVISION NOTES
- The Hilbert quadrature pair was corrected (z^-2 allpass sections with the proper
  sign, plus a sign fix on the imaginary part). Before this fix the two paths were
  not in quadrature, so IFT readings were rejected by the anchor gate and the output
  was effectively smoothed YIN. IFT_speed and the PLL phasor/sine outputs now
  respond to the true analytic phase, so expect different behavior from earlier
  patches.
- Added the yin_window_ms parameter and made the YIN confidence weighting a soft ramp
  instead of a hard gate at 0.35.
