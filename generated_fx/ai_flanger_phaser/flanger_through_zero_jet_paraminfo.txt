================================================================================
flanger_through_zero_jet — Parameter Information
================================================================================

WHAT IT IS
The old through-zero tape-flange trick: two taps either side of a reference
delay, swept so their spacing passes through zero. The comb notches run all the
way up and back instead of just sliding between two delay times, which is the
sweeping "jet" sound — far more violent than an ordinary flanger, and the reason
this one is the go-to for liquid bass glides and long trip-hop drones.

Source: Huovilainen, DAFx-05 (2005_P_155) §1.1 and §4; flanger block after
Esqueda/Välimäki/Parker, DAFx-15 §3 Fig. 6; cubic Lagrange fractional delay
after Nam, DAFx-09 paper 72 §2.

100% wet: both taps are delayed, so no dry copy of the tuba reaches the output.
No dry/wet control, no limiter.

PARAMETERS (5 + output gain)

1. sweep_ms — 0.05…12 ms, default 2.2 ms
   Half-spread of the two taps. Small = tight metallic sheen on the midrange;
   large = slow, wide whoosh that reaches down into the low bass. This is the
   "size of the jet".

2. sweep_rate_hz — 0.02…2 Hz, default 0.14 Hz
   Speed of the sweep. Below 0.1 Hz it becomes a texture you hear as movement
   rather than as an LFO — good under long dubstep notes.

3. feedback — -0.95…+0.95, default -0.55
   Loop gain around the delay line. Negative is the classic hollow,
   out-of-phase flange; positive is thicker and more resonant. Values near
   ±0.9 whistle — that is the flanger on the edge, use it deliberately.

4. stereo_offset — 0…1, default 0.25
   Phase offset between the left and right sweep (0.25 = quarter cycle).
   At 0 the two channels are identical and the effect sits in the middle.

5. tone_hz — 800…14000 Hz, default 7000 Hz
   One-pole damping inside the feedback loop. Lower keeps the resonance dark
   and dubby; higher lets the metal ring.

6. out_gain_db — -24…+12 dB, default 0 dB
   Final trim. Does not count toward the 8-parameter limit.

INLETS / OUTLETS
- in1 / in2: stereo audio.
- in3: connected but unused — this sweep is time-based, not locked to the note.
- in4: one-bar phasor. While it moves, the sweep follows the bar; held at 0 the
  effect free-runs at sweep_rate_hz. Either way it is quiet-safe.
- out1 / out2: stereo, wet only.

NOTES
- The two taps are centred on a 0.15 ms floor, so the relative delay crosses
  zero (through-zero) while neither tap ever reads the input directly.
- Loop damping and the ±0.95 feedback clamp keep the IIR comb bounded.
