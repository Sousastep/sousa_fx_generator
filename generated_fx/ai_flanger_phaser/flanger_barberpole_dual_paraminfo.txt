================================================================================
flanger_barberpole_dual — Parameter Information
================================================================================

WHAT IT IS
A barberpole flanger: the notches keep climbing (or falling) and never arrive.
Two delay lines are swept by sawtooth ramps half a cycle apart, and a triangular
crossfade hands over from one to the other at the exact moment its ramp resets,
so the reset is inaudible. On a sustained bass note the movement simply never
resolves — no "the LFO came round again" moment.

Source: Esqueda, Välimäki & Parker, DAFx-15 §3, Fig. 6–8, Eq. 9.

100% wet (no dry/wet control). The output is the flanger sum x + g1·D1 + g2·D2;
there is no dry blend knob and no limiter.

PARAMETERS (7 + output gain)

1. sweep_rate_hz — 0.02…1.5 Hz, default 0.09 Hz
   How fast the notches travel. The illusion needs slow: above ~0.5 Hz the ear
   starts hearing the cycle instead of endless motion.

2. max_delay_ms — 2…14 ms, default 7 ms
   Longest delay = lowest first notch. Short values give a tight metallic
   shimmer; long values reach into the bass and sound like a slow jet.

3. min_ratio — 0.5…0.95, default 0.72
   Shortest delay as a fraction of the longest. Keep it above 0.5, otherwise the
   hand-over between the two lines becomes audible (this is the paper's Dmin >
   Dmax/2 condition).

4. direction — falling / rising, default rising
   Rising = notches climb (delay gets shorter over the cycle). Falling is the
   same illusion backwards — good for downward "sinking" drops.

5. comb_depth — 0…1, default 0.85
   How much delayed signal is added. At 0 the output is untouched input; at 1
   the notches are as deep as the crossfade allows.

6. feedback — -0.9…+0.9, default +0.4
   Recirculation around the delay line. Positive sharpens the peaks and makes it
   resonant; negative gives the hollow out-of-phase flange. High values whistle.

7. stereo_spread — 0…1, default 0.3
   Phase offset between the two channels' cycles (0.5 = half a cycle apart, fully
   decorrelated). At 0 both channels move together and the effect is mono.

8. out_gain_db — -24…+12 dB, default 0 dB
   Final trim. Does not count toward the 8-parameter limit.

INLETS / OUTLETS
- in1 / in2: stereo audio.
- in3: connected but unused — the sweep is time-based, not pitch-locked.
- in4: one-bar phasor. While it moves it advances the sweep cycle; held at 0 the
  effect free-runs at sweep_rate_hz. Quiet-safe either way.
- out1 / out2: stereo, wet only.

NOTES
- Crossfade gains are complementary triangles (g1 + g2 = 1 at every phase), so
  the hand-over cannot dip the level.
- A fixed 6 kHz one-pole sits inside the feedback path; the delay span is clamped
  to 1400 samples so the buffer cannot be overrun at high sample rates.
