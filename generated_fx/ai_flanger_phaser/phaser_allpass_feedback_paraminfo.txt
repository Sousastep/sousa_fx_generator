================================================================================
phaser_allpass_feedback — Parameter Information
================================================================================

WHAT IT IS
The straight-ahead phaser, done properly: a chain of identical first-order
allpass sections mixed against the input, with a feedback loop around the chain
for the resonance peaks. That is the pedal topology (MXR / Small Stone / Uni-Vibe
family) rather than a flanger with a phase-shifted face — the notches are not
harmonically spaced but spread roughly evenly in log-frequency around the break
frequency fb (0.20·fb, 0.67·fb, 1.5·fb, 5.0·fb for 8 stages), so they move as
a group across octaves, which is the sound you hear as "liquid".

Source: Kiiski, Esqueda & Välimäki, DAFx-16 paper 42 §2, Eq. 1–2, Fig. 1;
Huovilainen, DAFx-05 §2–3; Eichas et al., DAFx-14 (MXR Phase 90).

100% wet. No dry/wet control, no limiter.

PARAMETERS (7)

1. notches — 2…8, default 4
   Number of notches (two allpass stages each). 2 = broad, vocal, one moving
   hollow. 4 = the classic pedal. 8 = dense, metallic, close to a flanger but
   with harmonically spread notches.

2. center_hz — 100…3000 Hz, default 520 Hz
   Middle of the sweep. Low (100–250 Hz) puts the notches on the tuba's
   fundamental and low harmonics — a slow, physical "breathing" of the note.
   High pulls the sweep into the brass formants.

3. sweep_octaves — 0…4, default 2
   How far the sweep travels, total, across the note. At 0 the phaser is static
   (a fixed resonant comb — useful as a formant); at 4 it goes from sub to
   sizzle.

4. rate_hz — 0.02…3 Hz, default 0.22 Hz
   Sweep speed. Below ~0.1 Hz on a long note it reads as texture, not tremolo.

5. feedback — 0…0.9, default 0.35
   Loop gain around the allpass chain. This is what turns the notches into
   resonant peaks; above ~0.7 the peaks whistle and start to dominate. The loop
   also resonates at DC, so it lifts the sub: about +2 dB at 0.35, +5 dB at
   0.6 and +15 dB at 0.9 on the lowest frequencies. Trim with out_gain_db.

6. stereo_phase — 0…1, default 0.25
   Phase offset between the two channels' sweeps. 1 = half a cycle (the two
   sides sweep in opposite directions, widest), 0.5 = quarter cycle (default).
   At 0 the phaser is mono.

7. out_gain_db — -24…+12 dB, default 0 dB
   Final trim.

INLETS / OUTLETS
- in1 / in2: stereo audio.
- in3: connected but unused — this phaser sweeps its own centre, not the note.
- in4: 0..1 one-bar phasor (not Hz). While it moves the sweep runs exactly one
  full cycle per bar and rate_hz is ignored; held constant or unconnected the
  effect free-runs at rate_hz. A stepped (non-phasor) signal here will jump
  the sweep phase.
- out1 / out2: stereo, wet only.

NOTES
- The stages are identical, as in the pedal, so one tan() per channel per sample
  sets the whole cascade.
- Output is 0.5·(input + chain): the deepest possible notches. Unity at DC only
  with feedback at 0 (see feedback above).
- Feedback is capped at 0.9 and the coefficients stay inside the unit circle for
  any break frequency, so the loop cannot run away.
