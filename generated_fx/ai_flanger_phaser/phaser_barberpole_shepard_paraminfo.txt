================================================================================
phaser_barberpole_shepard — Parameter Information
================================================================================

WHAT IT IS
A barberpole *phaser* (not a flanger): the notches keep travelling up (or down)
and never return. It works the way Shepard's endless scale works — a ladder of
notches one octave apart, with a raised-cosine depth envelope, so the notch that
leaves the top is replaced by the one arriving at the bottom. The ear hears
endless motion; the spectrum is actually periodic.

Because phasers place their notches a musical distance apart rather than every
few Hz like a flanger, this one sweeps as a slow, vocal "warp" rather than a jet.
It is the sound for trip-hop and space-bass beds that must never settle.

Source: Esqueda, Välimäki & Parker, DAFx-15 §2, Eq. 1–8, Fig. 2–5.

100% wet: the output is the cascade of notch filters itself (no dry blend, no
mix knob, no limiter).

PARAMETERS (6 + output gain)

1. notch_rate_hz — 0.01…0.3 Hz, default 0.06 Hz
   Ladder speed. The illusion needs very slow — the paper found it breaks down
   above ~0.3 Hz.

2. ladder_base_hz — 20…400 Hz, default 63 Hz
   Bottom notch of the ladder. Low values stack the notches across the bass and
   take the movement below the fundamental; higher values pull the action into
   the tuba's formant region.

3. stages — 4…10, default 8
   Number of notches in the ladder. More = denser comb of notches and a
   smoother illusion; fewer = a more obvious, hollow, "vowelly" sweep.

4. notch_q — 2…40, default 12
   Notch width. Low Q is a broad, gentle dip; high Q is a narrow whistle that
   only bites the harmonics, which is what makes it audible on a pure bass note.

5. notch_depth_db — 3…36 dB, default 18 dB
   Deepest notch, applied at the middle of the ladder. The ends are always
   shallower (3 dB), which is what lets notches enter and leave unnoticed.

6. direction — falling / rising, default rising
   Rising climbs; falling sinks. Both are endless.

7. out_gain_db — -24…+12 dB, default 0 dB
   Final trim. Does not count toward the 8-parameter limit.

INLETS / OUTLETS
- in1 / in2: stereo audio.
- in3: connected but unused — the ladder is anchored by ladder_base_hz.
- in4: one-bar phasor. While it moves it advances the ladder; held at 0 the
  effect free-runs at notch_rate_hz.
- out1 / out2: stereo, wet only.

NOTES
- The right channel runs the same ladder offset by a quarter of a stage, so the
  two sides sit a quarter-octave apart and the image is wide.
- The ladder is periodic in phase, so the cycle wraps with no reset click and no
  state carry-over (which the table-based implementation in the paper needs).
- Each notch is y = x − (1 − G)·bp/Q, giving exactly G at fc for any Q.
