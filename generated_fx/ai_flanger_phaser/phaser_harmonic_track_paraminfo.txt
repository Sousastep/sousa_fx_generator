================================================================================
phaser_harmonic_track — Parameter Information
================================================================================

WHAT IT IS
A phaser whose notches land exactly on the partials you choose of the note you
are playing. Pick the lowest partial to notch and the spacing, and every
notch sits dead on its partial — 2, 3, 4; or the odd ones 1, 3, 5…; or every
third, 3, 6, 9… — and glides with the tuba as you change notes. Notching
chosen partials of a real instrument changes its colour the way a mute or a
different bore would: notch the odd partials and the tuba turns hollow and
octave-up; notch the evens and it goes square and woody; notch 2-3-4 and the
body drops out leaving fundamental and buzz.

How it works: each notch is its own two-stage phaser cell — the input mixed
50/50 with a second-order allpass centred on the partial. The allpass phase is
exactly −180° at its centre, so the cell cancels that frequency completely,
whatever the other cells do. (A classic chain of first-order stages can't do
this: its notches fall where the *summed* phase reaches 180°, 540°, …, so they
can't be placed independently, and they can't sit on neighbouring harmonics at
all.) Measured on a 16-harmonic test tone, with the wobble off: the chosen
partials come out at −214 dB (the numerical floor) and the others are
untouched at 0 dB.

Source: Kiiski, Esqueda & Välimäki, DAFx-16 paper 42 §2, Eq. 1–2, Fig. 1
(50/50 phaser mix, notch where the allpass reaches −180°); Huovilainen,
DAFx-05 §2–3 (allpass cascade phaser); Carson et al., DAFx-23 paper 38 §2
(time-varying allpass cascade).

100% wet: the notch cascade is the whole signal. No dry/wet control, no
limiter. Every cell's gain is ≤ 1 at all frequencies, so it never boosts.

PARAMETERS (8)

1. lowest_partial — 1…16, default 2
   The first partial to notch (1 = the fundamental, 2 = the octave, 3 = the
   twelfth, …).

2. partial_step — 1…8, default 1
   Spacing between notched partials. With lowest_partial 2: step 1 notches 2,
   3, 4…; step 2 notches the evens 2, 4, 6…. With lowest_partial 1 and step 2
   it notches the odd partials 1, 3, 5…. Step 3 from 3 gives 3, 6, 9….

3. notches — 1…6, default 3
   How many partials are notched: lowest_partial, then each partial_step above
   it. Notches above 0.40 × sample rate fade out (gone by 0.45 × sample rate).

4. notch_width — 0.05…1 (× f0), default 0.5
   Notch bandwidth as a fraction of the note's pitch, the same for every notch,
   so each notch is equally selective relative to the harmonic spacing. The
   centre is always a complete null; width decides how much of the space
   around it is cut — narrow = surgical (only the partial goes, breath and
   noise around it stay), wide = broad phaser-like hollows that also catch
   the partial while the wobble or a bend moves the notch off it. At 1 the
   neighbouring notches of step 1 merge into a band cut.

5. glide_ms — 5…500 ms, default 40 ms
   How fast the notches follow a new note. Short is immediate and can chirp on
   a fast line; long makes the notches lag the tuba like a slow filter
   envelope. The notches are exact once the glide has settled.

6. sweep_octaves — 0…1, default 0.1
   Depth of a slow wobble that moves all notches together around their
   partials (±half the value in octaves). 0 = locked exactly on the partials;
   small values make the cut partials breathe in and out; larger values sweep
   the notches between partials like a conventional phaser.

7. rate_hz — 0.02…2 Hz, default 0.15 Hz
   Wobble rate. The right channel's wobble runs a quarter cycle ahead of the
   left, which is what makes it stereo (at sweep_octaves 0 it is mono).

8. out_gain_db — -24…+12 dB, default 0 dB
   Final trim.

INLETS / OUTLETS
- in1 / in2: stereo audio.
- in3: f0 in Hz. 0 or out of range = unknown: the last valid value is held.
  Cold start 55 Hz (A1), so the notches are placed with nothing patched.
- in4: 0..1 one-bar phasor (not Hz). While it moves the wobble runs exactly one
  full cycle per bar and rate_hz is ignored; held constant or unconnected the
  effect free-runs at rate_hz. A stepped (non-phasor) signal here will jump
  the wobble phase.
- out1 / out2: stereo, wet only.

NOTES
- Redesign: earlier versions put first-order allpass break frequencies on a
  5-limit ladder and claimed the notches sat on the partials; they actually
  fell between them (≈0.57, 2.45, 10.6 × f0 at the old defaults). The old
  params track_octave, ladder_span, stage_count and feedback are gone.
- No feedback control on purpose: a loop around this cascade keeps the notches
  exact, but since everything away from the notches passes at ~unity with
  almost no phase shift, the loop only raised or lowered the overall level
  (+6…+8 dB / −4 dB at ±0.6) instead of making resonant peaks.
- Accuracy depends on the tracker: the notches sit on the partials of the f0
  that arrives at in3, so a tracker that is off by some cents moves the
  notches off the partials by the same amount.
- There is no internal pitch detector: f0 comes from in3 only, and 0 is treated
  as "unknown", never as 1 Hz.
- Each cell is a TPT state-variable notch (x − k·bp, which equals
  0.5·(x + allpass)), prewarped with tan() so the null is exact at h·f0.
