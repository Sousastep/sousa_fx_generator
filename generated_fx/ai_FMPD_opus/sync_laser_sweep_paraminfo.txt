================================================================================
sync_laser_sweep — Parameter Information
================================================================================

WHAT IT IS
The space-bass laser "pew". A synth oscillator is hard-synced to the tuba's
own phase, so it always plays your note, and its sync ratio dives from high
to low on every bar step: the classic sync sweep. It follows your dynamics
and blends with the horn without phasing, because it shares the horn's
period and phase.

Source: DAFx-12 paper 37 §2–3 (virtual analog hard sync and its
band-limited reset); Timoney, Lazzarini, Gibney & Pekonen, DAFx-10 P65;
Kleimola, Lazzarini, Timoney & Välimäki, DAFx-11 paper 55 §5.
Parent: 04_hilbert_phasor_sync.

PARAMETERS (7 + output gain)

1. sync_lo — 1…8, default 1
   Sync ratio at the end of each sweep (1 = plain saw at the note).

2. sync_hi — 1…16, default 6
   Sync ratio at the start of each sweep (bright, nasal peak). Lower than
   sync_lo = a rising sweep.

3. sweeps_per_bar — 1…16 (stepped), default 2
   Laser hits per bar.

4. curve — -100…+100 %, default +60 %
   + = drops fast then lingers low ("pew"); - = lingers high then drops;
   0 = even exponential sweep.

5. soft_hard — 0…100 %, default 100 %
   100 = hard sync (buzzy, band-limited saw reset); 0 = soft, windowed
   sine sync (round, vocal).

6. tone_hz — 200…12000 Hz, default 5000 Hz
   Two-pole lowpass on the laser.

7. blend — 0…100 %, default 50 %
   Horn/laser crossfade. Phase-locked, so 50 % doesn't comb.

8. out_gain_db — -24…+12 dB, default 0 dB
   Final trim. Does not count toward the 8-parameter limit.

INLETS / OUTLETS
- in1 / in2: stereo tuba.
- in3: f0 in Hz. 0 = disconnected -> fixed 55 Hz. No zero-crossing fallback.
  Only used when in5 is not patched.
- in4: one-bar phasor (sweep steps). If held, an internal 2 s bar (120 BPM)
  runs.
- in5: phase-locked tuba phasor (sync master). If static, an internal ramp
  at f0 carries on from the last phase.
- in6: smoothed tuba envelope (laser level). If static, an internal
  follower is used.
- out1 / out2: stereo (R runs a 3 % higher ratio for width).

NOTES
- PolyBLEP corrections at every slave wrap and master reset (one sample of
  latency; the dry is delayed to match). In an offline check at 220 Hz,
  ratio 6.3, they lowered the aliased energy from -16 dB to -36 dB below
  the harmonics.
- The laser has a 10 Hz DC blocker.
- No safety limiter.
