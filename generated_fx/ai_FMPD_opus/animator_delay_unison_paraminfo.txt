================================================================================
animator_delay_unison — Parameter Information
================================================================================

WHAT IT IS
A supersaw for the real horn. Pairs of delay-line pitch shifters make copies
of the tuba a few cents sharp and flat, which drift slowly with the bar, and
they surround the untouched dry horn in the middle. This is the "waveform
animator" trick that makes one oscillator sound like a stack of detuned saws.
The copies are high-passed so the sub stays clean and mono.

Source: Timoney, Lazzarini, Pekonen & Välimäki, DAFx-14 §2, §5 eq. 24–25
(animator from delay-line pitch shifters); Disch & Zölzer, DAFx-99 §3;
Hyrkas & Smyth, DAFx-24 paper 84 §2 (delay slope = frequency ratio).
Parent: 05_phaseshaper_supersaw_tri.

PARAMETERS (7 + output gain)

1. detune_cents — 0…50 cents, default 12
   Detune of the outermost pair; inner pairs are spaced evenly inside it.
   5–15 = lush unison; 30–50 = sour, wide Reese.

2. voice_pairs — 1…4 (stepped), default 3
   Each pair is one sharp and one flat copy (2–8 voices).

3. drift_per_bar — 0.25…4 cycles per bar, default 0.5
   Speed of the slow detune drift (±20 % of each voice's detune), counted
   over a 4-bar cycle.

4. window_periods — 2…8 (stepped), default 4
   Shifter grain length in f0 periods. Short = tighter, more "buzzy";
   long = smoother but more smeared and delayed (up to ~270 ms at pedal
   Bb). Held while the horn is silent.

5. width — 0…100 %, default 80 %
   Spreads sharp and flat copies to opposite sides, alternating per pair.

6. sub_mono_hz — 40…250 Hz, default 120 Hz
   The copies are high-passed below this; the lows are only the dry horn.

7. blend — 0…100 %, default 60 %
   Brings the copies in. The dry horn always stays as the centre voice
   (it dips by 4 dB at 100 %), so there is never a dry/wet comb on the sub.

8. out_gain_db — -24…+12 dB, default 0 dB
   Final trim. Does not count toward the 8-parameter limit.

INLETS / OUTLETS
- in1 / in2: stereo tuba (the copies read the mid signal).
- in3: f0 in Hz. 0 = disconnected -> fixed 55 Hz. No zero-crossing fallback.
- in4: one-bar phasor (drift). If held, an internal 2 s bar (120 BPM) runs.
- in5: pitch confidence (0 while silent, ~1 while playing). While it is low
  the grain length is held at the last note's value. Never moved since
  load = unpatched = treated as 1.
- out1 / out2: stereo.

NOTES
- The copies are delayed by up to one window, so they ring on briefly after
  you stop; that is the shifter's buffer, not a bug.
- No safety limiter; at 4 pairs, 50 cents and 100 % blend, peaks rise about
  +3 dB.
