================================================================================
expfm_tvdl_growl — Parameter Information
================================================================================

WHAT IT IS
Analog-style exponential FM applied to the whole horn through a wobbling
delay line: the dark, lopsided, throaty growl of a modular through-zero-less
FM patch. It is re-tuned so the note stays in pitch, the modulator runs on
the tuba's own phase, and the depth hits and decays on each bar step. Turn
re-tuning down and every step becomes a pitch swoop.

Source: DAFx-20 paper 61 §4–4.3 (exponential FM, its DC offset
I0(alpha ln 2), re-tuning); Smyth, DAFx-26 paper 36 §2 (FM with a
time-varying delay line); Hyrkas & Smyth, DAFx-24 paper 84 §2.
Parent: retuned_exp_fm_ltz.

PARAMETERS (7 + output gain)

1. exp_octaves — 0…3 octaves, default 0.8
   Peak frequency swing of the horn, in octaves up and down (exponential).
   0 = clean.

2. mod_ratio — 0.5…4 × f0, default 1
   Modulator frequency, snapped to 0.5 steps. 1 = harmonic growl; 0.5 =
   sub-octave growl; 1.5 / 2.5 = hollow, nasal.

3. retune — 0…100 %, default 100 %
   100 = fully re-tuned: exponential FM's built-in pitch drift is divided
   out and the note stays in tune. Lower = part of the drift is left in;
   since a delay cannot hold a pitch change, it shows up as a swoop on every
   depth change that settles back in tune within ~60 ms.

4. bar_depth — 0…100 %, default 50 %
   How much the depth decays through each step (hits on the step, then
   closes). 0 = steady.

5. depth_div — 1…16 (stepped), default 2
   Depth steps per bar.

6. tone_hz — 200…12000 Hz, default 4000 Hz
   Two-pole lowpass on the wet.

7. blend — 0…100 %, default 100 %
   Dry/wet. The dry is read from the same delay line at the same centre
   delay, so parallel blends do not comb.

8. out_gain_db — -24…+12 dB, default 0 dB
   Final trim. Does not count toward the 8-parameter limit.

INLETS / OUTLETS
- in1 / in2: stereo tuba.
- in3: f0 in Hz. 0 = disconnected -> fixed 55 Hz. No zero-crossing fallback.
  Only used when in5 is not patched.
- in4: one-bar phasor (depth steps). If held, an internal 2 s bar (120 BPM)
  runs.
- in5: phase-locked tuba phasor (modulator). If static, an internal ramp at
  f0 carries on from the last phase.
- out1 / out2: stereo.

NOTES
- Latency = the centre delay, which follows the deepest swing: about 2 ms
  at the defaults on A1, ~20 ms at 3 octaves with ratio 0.5. It rises at
  once and relaxes over about half a second.
- No through-zero: the horn never plays backwards, so the delay is always
  causal.
- The wet path has a 10 Hz DC blocker.
- No safety limiter.
