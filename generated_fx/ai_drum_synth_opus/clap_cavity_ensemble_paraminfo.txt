================================================================================
clap_cavity_ensemble — Parameter Information
================================================================================

WHAT IT IS
A triggered hand clap built like real hands. Each clap is a ~5 ms noise
burst into the resonance of the air pocket between the palms: cupped hands
ring low and hollow (~500 Hz), flat hands high and sharp (~1.5 kHz). Up to
twenty virtual clappers fire together, flammed, or spread over up to a
second. Each one cups a little differently and is re-drawn on every hit from measured
hand-clap statistics, so no two claps match. A filtered noise tail (a
short room, or the long smeared tail of an 808 clap) follows each clap; a
tight flam shares one tail after its last clap.

Every hit also drifts the whole group's hand shape slightly (random walk).
A retrigger while the clap is still sounding flams tighter (the hands are
already close) and adds into what is ringing. Nothing resets. With long
spreads, a new hit reschedules the claps of the previous one that have
not landed yet (the new clap restarts the group).

Source: Jylhä & Erkut, DAFx-08 §2-2.1 and Table 1 (ClaPD: per-clap
resonator f / B / G drawn per hand configuration); Ahmad & Kondoz, DAFx-11
§5.2, §6.1 (non-repetitive impact sounds).

PARAMETERS (7 + output gain)

1. cavity — 0…100 %, default 50 %
   Hand shape, following the paper's configurations: cupped A1+ (~500 Hz)
   -> A1 -> A1- -> A2 -> flat P3 (~1560 Hz). Low = hollow, dubby; high =
   snappy, cutting.

2. clappers — 1…20 (stepped), default 4
   People clapping. 1 = a single dry clap; 4-6 = a thick group clap;
   12-20 = a crowd. Loudness is normalised to how many claps overlap, so
   it stays about the same (peaks within ~4 dB of the default).

3. spread_ms — 0…1000 ms, default 12 ms
   How far apart the clappers land (tapered: the first third of the knob
   covers 0-40 ms). 0 = one fat hit; 10-15 = classic drum-machine flam;
   30-80 = loose, human group; 300-1000 with many clappers = a burst of
   applause. Claps more than 30 ms apart each get their own tail; a
   tighter flam shares one.

4. tail_ms — 20…1500 ms, default 250 ms
   Noise tail after each clap (time to -40 dB). Short = dry room; long
   = big trip-hop / space reverb clap.

5. air — 0…100 %, default 50 %
   Palm-slap brightness on each clap, and how open the tail is. 0 = dark,
   resonant only; 100 = crisp and splashy.

6. width — 0…100 %, default 60 %
   Clapper panning and tail decorrelation. 0 = mono.

7. variance — 0…100 %, default 35 %
   Scales the paper's per-clap deviations and the drift between hits.
   0 = every clapper exactly on the mean (their timing and personal hand
   bias still differ).

8. out_gain_db — -24…+12 dB, default 0 dB
   Final trim, last in the chain.

INLETS / OUTLETS
- in1: trigger. A single-sample click (any rising edge above 0.1). No
  velocity: the level is fixed, and variance supplies the movement.
- out1 / out2: stereo.

NOTES
- Isolated hits peak around -7…-3 dBFS at defaults. Long tails pile up
  when rolled fast (+7 dBFS with tail_ms 1500 and air 100).
- No safety limiter.
