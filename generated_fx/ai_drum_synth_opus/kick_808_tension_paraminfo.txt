================================================================================
kick_808_tension — Parameter Information
================================================================================

WHAT IT IS
A triggered kick. A sine body starts sharp and falls to its pitch as its
energy drains, like a tensioned head (or an 808's bridged-T). At the hit the
waveform carries a loopback-FM harmonic series, so the kick still reads on
phone speakers. The series thins to a clean sine as the note dies, which
keeps the low end tight instead of boomy. A soft contact pulse gives a felt
beater thump plus a mid "knock", lowpassed so it never clicks.

Every hit lands on a slightly different spot (detune, brightness, knock
pitch, contact time drift by a random walk). Hitting a drum that is still
ringing ramps from where it is: the pitch starts a little higher (energy
adds) and the contact is shorter and softer. No reset, no click.

Source: Werner, Abel & Smith, DAFx-14 (TR-808 bass drum: §4 pulse shaper,
§8.1 attack frequency shift, §8.2 pitch sigh); Marogna, Avanzini & Bank,
DAFx-10 P49 §2.3, §3.2 eq. 20 (tension ~ energy); Hsu & Smyth, SMC-19 §3.2,
§3.4 eq. 8-9 (closed-form loopback FM); Avanzini & Rocchesso, DAFx-01 §2.1
(contact force).

PARAMETERS (7 + output gain)

1. tune_hz — 35…800 Hz, default 50 Hz
   Pitch the body settles to. 45-55 = deep dubstep; 60-70 = trip-hop thud;
   150+ = tom-like, then synth-blip territory. The level is compensated
   across the range (K-weighted loudness within 3.6 dB from 35 to 800 Hz),
   and the harmonics thin out at high tunings so they never alias.

2. decay_ms — 80…1500 ms, default 380 ms
   Time to -40 dB. Under 250 = tight and punchy; over 800 = 808-style boom.

3. drop_st — 0…36 st, default 14 st
   How far above tune_hz the hit starts. 0 = flat sine; 24+ = laser zap.

4. drop_ms — 5…200 ms, default 35 ms
   How fast the pitch falls. Short = punch; long = sighing, dubby kick.

5. harmonics — 0…100 %, default 45 %
   Loopback-FM brightness at the attack, plus gentle saturation. This is
   what phones and laptops hear. 0 = pure sine.

6. beater — 0…100 %, default 40 %
   Beater level and hardness: soft felt thump to hard plastic knock.

7. variance — 0…100 %, default 35 %
   How much the strike spot wanders from hit to hit. 0 = identical hits.

8. out_gain_db — -24…+12 dB, default 0 dB
   Final trim, last in the chain.

INLETS / OUTLETS
- in1: trigger. A single-sample click (any rising edge above 0.1). No
  velocity: the level is fixed, and variance supplies the movement.
- out1 / out2: the kick (mono, same on both).

NOTES
- Isolated hits peak near -2 dBFS at defaults; fast rolls stack higher.
- No safety limiter.
