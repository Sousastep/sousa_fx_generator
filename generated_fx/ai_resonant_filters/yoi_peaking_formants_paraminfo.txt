================================================================================
yoi_peaking_formants — Parameter Information
================================================================================

WHAT IT IS
A formant EQ: three resonant peaking bells boost the first three formants
of a vowel on top of the full signal, instead of filtering everything
else away. The tuba keeps all of its body and low end while the vowel
glides U -> O -> I with in3. Resonance raises the boost and narrows the
bells together; at 0 the effect is a flat passthrough.

Source: Liski & Välimäki, DAFx-17 paper 94 §2 (second-order peaking / bell
filters); Traube & D'Alessandro, DAFx-05 P_104 §1.5 (vowels in the (F1, F2)
plane).

PARAMETERS (4)

1. max_boost_db — 6…24 dB, default 15 dB
   Formant boost at full resonance. 6 dB = a subtle vowel tint; 24 dB =
   loud, whistling talk-box formants.

2. formant_shift_st — -12…+12 semitones, default 0
   Moves all three formants together without changing the vowel: down =
   bigger, darker mouth; up = smaller, brighter.

3. freq_offset_st — -36…+36 semitones, default 0
   Offsets the vowel position that arrives on in3. 24 st moves from U to
   O, 48 st from U to I.

4. width — 0…100 %, default 50 %
   Adds a side signal from a copy with its formants shifted up to +1 octave
   (at 100 %). L+R is exactly the plain filter, so mono is unaffected.

INLETS / OUTLETS
- in1 / in2: stereo audio.  out1 / out2: stereo.
- in3: vowel position as a frequency in Hz, log scale: 200 Hz or lower =
  U, 800 Hz = O, 3200 Hz or higher = I. Values <= 1 Hz are ignored (last
  value held, 800 Hz = O at start).
- in4: resonance 0…1: boost = in4 × max_boost_db, bell Q = 1…8. 0 or
  unpatched = flat, dry passthrough.

NOTES
- Formants (Hz): U 300 / 870 / 2240, O 570 / 840 / 2410, I 270 / 2290 /
  3010, interpolated in log frequency; all three get the same boost.
- Gain staging: the output takes back a fifth of the boost (about the
  average boost the tuba actually receives, since the bells mostly miss
  its strong low harmonics). With the test tuba, resonance 0 to 1 changes
  the level by +2.4 dB (24 dB boost at U, mono) to -3.8 dB.
- in3 / in4 are smoothed over 5 ms.
- No safety limiter.
