================================================================================
yoi_two_formant_svf — Parameter Information
================================================================================

WHAT IT IS
The synth-patch "yoi": two classic state-variable filters with saturating
band integrators. One is a resonant lowpass sitting on the first formant,
the other a resonant bandpass on the second, and both glide U -> O -> I as
in3 rises. Two formants are enough to hear the vowel. The saturation gives
the gritty, rubbery tone of a hardware filter pushed hard.

Source: Wise, DAFx-06 p053 §1–2 (Chamberlin SVF structure, lowpass and
bandpass outputs, stability range); Han, Na & Lee, NIME-12 (two formants
suffice for vowels); McClellan, DAFx-26 paper 28 §SVF.

PARAMETERS (4)

1. drive_db — 0…24 dB, default 6 dB
   Level into the saturating integrators. More = grittier, more compressed
   formants. The output is trimmed by 3/4 of the drive in dB.

2. f1_level_db — -12…+6 dB, default 0 dB
   Level of the F1 lowpass (which carries the tuba's body) against the F2
   bandpass. Turn it down for a thin, nasal, talking-only sound (much
   quieter overall, since tuba energy sits low).

3. freq_offset_st — -36…+36 semitones, default 0
   Offsets the vowel position that arrives on in3. 24 st moves from U to
   O, 48 st from U to I.

4. width — 0…100 %, default 50 %
   Adds a side signal from a copy with both formants shifted up to +1
   octave (at 100 %). L+R is exactly the plain filter, so mono is
   unaffected.

INLETS / OUTLETS
- in1 / in2: stereo audio.  out1 / out2: stereo.
- in3: vowel position as a frequency in Hz, log scale: 200 Hz or lower =
  U, 800 Hz = O, 3200 Hz or higher = I. Values <= 1 Hz are ignored (last
  value held, 800 Hz = O at start).
- in4: resonance 0…1 = Q 1…20 (log taper), both filters.

NOTES
- Formants (Hz): F1 / F2 = U 300 / 870, O 570 / 840, I 270 / 2290,
  interpolated in log frequency.
- Gain staging: the lowpass passband stays at unity and the saturated band
  state caps its peak; the bandpass is unity-peak × sqrt(Q) (constant
  power). With the test tuba, resonance 0 to 1 changes the level by at most
  +2.3 dB at any drive.
- in3 / in4 are smoothed over 5 ms.
- No safety limiter.
