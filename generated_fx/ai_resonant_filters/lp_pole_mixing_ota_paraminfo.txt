================================================================================
lp_pole_mixing_ota — Parameter Information
================================================================================

WHAT IT IS
A 4-stage OTA one-pole cascade with resonance feedback, Xpander style: the
outputs of the five points along the cascade are mixed to give 24 dB
lowpass, 12 dB lowpass, 12 dB bandpass or 12 dB highpass, all with the same
resonance. One knob sweeps through them. The input stage saturates softly.

Source: Trausmuth & Huovilainen, DAFx-05 P_293 (pole mixing of ladder stage
outputs); Huovilainen, DAFx-04 P_061 §3–5 (input-stage saturation,
feedback up to 4); McClellan, DAFx-26 paper 28 (time-varying stability).

PARAMETERS (4)

1. shape — 0…1, default 0
   Response: 0 = LP4, 0.33 = LP2, 0.67 = BP2, 1 = HP2, crossfading in
   between. Note that tuba has little energy up high, so the BP/HP end is
   much quieter at high cutoffs. That is the filter working, not a level
   bug.

2. drive_db — 0…24 dB, default 3 dB
   Saturation at the cascade input. Small-signal gain stays at unity.

3. freq_offset_st — -36…+36 semitones, default 0
   Transposes the cutoff that arrives on in3.

4. width — 0…100 %, default 50 %
   Adds a side signal from a copy of the filter tuned up to +1 octave (at
   100 %). L+R is exactly the plain filter, so mono is unaffected.

INLETS / OUTLETS
- in1 / in2: stereo audio.  out1 / out2: stereo.
- in3: cutoff in Hz. Values <= 1 Hz are ignored (last value held, 600 Hz at
  start).
- in4: resonance 0…1 (feedback k = 4 × in4, 1 = edge of self-oscillation).

NOTES
- Gain staging: feedback cuts the lowpass passband by up to 14 dB. LP4 gets
  half of that back (sqrt(1 + k)), LP2 a quarter (its tap already rings
  6 dB more), BP/HP none. With the test tuba, resonance 0 to 1 raises the
  level by at most ~5 dB (LP4 with cutoff at 150 Hz) and lowers it at high
  cutoffs.
- in3 / in4 are smoothed over 5 ms.
- No safety limiter.
