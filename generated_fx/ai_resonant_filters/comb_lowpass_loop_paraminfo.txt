================================================================================
comb_lowpass_loop — Parameter Information
================================================================================

WHAT IT IS
A feedback comb filter tuned to the frequency on in3, with a one-pole
lowpass inside the loop (Karplus-Strong damping). It resonates at f, 2f,
3f… and the upper partials of the ring die away faster, so it sounds like a
plucked or bowed string sympathetically ringing with the tuba. The loop is
tuned with a fractional-delay allpass, so pitch is exact at any frequency.

Source: DAFx-98 tutorial (DUT52) §3 Fig. 6 (comb with lowpass in the loop);
Laurson, DAFx-00 §2 (loop gain and loop filter); Marttila et al., DAFx-26
paper 38 §2.1 (extended Karplus-Strong tuning with loss filter and
fractional delay).

PARAMETERS (3)

1. damping — 0…100 %, default 30 %
   Loop lowpass. 0 % = bright, metallic ring; 100 % = the loop lowpass sits
   on the fundamental, giving a dark, woody, short-lived ring.

2. freq_offset_st — -36…+36 semitones, default 0
   Transposes the comb pitch that arrives on in3 (try +12 or +19 with a
   pitch tracker on in3).

3. width — 0…100 %, default 50 %
   Adds a side signal from a second comb tuned up to +1 octave (at 100 %).
   L+R is exactly the plain comb, so mono is unaffected.

INLETS / OUTLETS
- in1 / in2: stereo audio.  out1 / out2: stereo.
- in3: comb fundamental in Hz (sr/9990…sr/4). Values <= 1 Hz are ignored
  (last value held, 600 Hz at start).
- in4: resonance 0…1 = loop gain 0…0.98. 0 or unpatched = the dry signal
  passes unchanged.

NOTES
- Gain staging: makeup is (1 - gE)^0.7 (1 + gE)^0.5, where gE is the loop
  gain at the fundamental after damping, sitting between power and peak
  normalisation. With the test tuba (58.7 Hz): a comb on the tuba's pitch
  rises at most ~6 dB in mono at full resonance; a comb that misses the
  tuba harmonics gets quieter (down to -20 dB at full resonance with low
  damping). That is what a comb does. Feed it the tracked pitch, or a
  multiple of it, for the loudest ring.
- in3 / in4 are smoothed over 5 ms.
- No safety limiter.
