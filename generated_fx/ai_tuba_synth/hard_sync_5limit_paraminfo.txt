================================================================================
hard_sync_5limit — Parameter Information
================================================================================

WHAT IT IS
The classic hard-sync bass voice: a master ramp at the tuba's pitch, a slave
oscillator running at a just ratio above it, and the slave forcibly reset on
every master cycle. The reset is what makes the "sync formant" — a bright,
vocal buzz that sits on the tuba's pitch instead of drifting away from it.
All ratios are 5-limit, so the voice is exactly in tune with the horn.

Source: Timoney, DAFx-12 (virtual-analog hard synchronisation, Fourier series
and efficient implementation).

The master ramp is the tuba's f0, so the whole waveform is exactly periodic at
f0. The voice is gated by the horn's envelope; at mix = 1 the tuba is replaced.

PARAMETERS (6 + output gain)

1. ratio — 0…3 (stepped), default 1
   0 = 3/2 (fifth above), 1 = 2/1 (octave), 2 = 3/1 (twelfth), 3 = 5/2 (just
   major tenth). Higher ratios make the sync formant brighter and more
   aggressive; 2/1 is the fat, usable "dubstep sync" default.

2. sync_shape — 0…1, default 0.3
   Morphs the slave from a saw (0) to a pulse (1). The pulse is thinner and
   more nasal; around 0.5 it has the hollow sync growl.

3. tone_hz — 200…12000 Hz, default 5000 Hz
   Two-pole lowpass after the oscillator. Lower for dark deep-dubstep weight,
   higher to let the sync edge bite.

4. drive — 0…1, default 0.3
   Soft saturation before the filter. Adds harmonics and a little level
   compression; 0 is clean.

5. bar_accent — 0…1, default 0.25
   Depth of a bar-locked accent/duck on the voice (and a small tone sweep).
   At 0 the voice is steady; higher gives it a rhythmic push against the kit.

6. mix — 0…1, default 0.9
   Dry tuba against the sync voice. The voice is phase-locked to the horn's
   f0 (and to in5 when patched), so the low end adds rather than cancels.

7. out_gain_db — -24…+12 dB, default 0 dB
   Final trim. Does not count toward the 8-parameter limit.

INLETS / OUTLETS
- in1 / in2: stereo tuba.
- in3: f0 in Hz. 0 or out-of-range means "unknown": the last valid pitch is
  held (init 55 Hz). No zero-crossing fallback.
- in4: one-bar phasor. Held at 0 the accent free-runs at 0.15 Hz.
- in5 (optional): phase-locked tuba phasor; when it moves the master ramp is
  pulled into phase with the horn.
- out1 / out2: stereo.

NOTES
- Saw and pulse are polyBLEP-corrected; the sync section runs 2x oversampled.
- No safety limiter.
