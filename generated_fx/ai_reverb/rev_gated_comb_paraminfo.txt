================================================================================
rev_gated_comb — Parameter Information
================================================================================

WHAT IT IS
A gated reverb: six parallel comb filters build a dense body, and a gate cuts
that body dead a fixed time after the hit that started it. The gate acts both
on the combs' feedback and on the output, so nothing is left ringing between
hits.

The gate is triggered by the input, not by the bar clock: play a note and the
tail opens, then cuts. Play again and it opens again. A long sustained note is
not retriggered, so it gets one body and then silence — that is the gated
character.

Source: "Digital Signal Processing Techniques for Non-exponentially Decaying
Reverberation", DAFx-98 Paper 20 §2.4 — a modified comb whose recirculating
impulse is shaped by an envelope synchronised to the delay length. Here the
envelope is the triggered gate and its length is a subdivision of the bar on
in4.

100% wet, no dry/wet. The 80s snare sound, and on tuba a short "room hit" that
stops on its own.

PARAMETERS (6 + output gain)

1. gate_div — 1/16, 1/8T, 1/8, 1/4, 1/2, 1 bar; default 1/8
   How long the tail stays open after each hit, as a subdivision of the bar on
   in4. 1/16 is a stab, 1/8 the classic gated length, 1/2 and 1 bar are long
   swells that still cut.

2. predelay_ms — 0…120 ms, default 10 ms
   Gap before the comb bank, and before the gate's sidechain, so the body
   arrives with the gate already open. 0–20 ms keeps the hit tight; 50 ms+
   gives a slap before the gate.

3. gate_shape — 0…1, default 0.85
   0 = the body fades −60 dB across the open gate (almost a normal reverb);
   1 = the body stays flat until the gate cuts it. This is the knob that goes
   from "reverb" to "gated".

4. tone_hz — 500…12000 Hz, default 3500 Hz
   One-pole lowpass in every comb loop. Low = dark, thick gated snare;
   high = bright and splashy.

5. diffuse — 0…1, default 0.5
   Gain of the two allpasses after the comb bank (0.50…0.85). Low leaves the
   comb pattern audible as flutter; high smears it into a smooth body.

6. width — 0…1, default 0.9
   Mid/side spread of the two different allpass pairs (left 5.3/1.9 ms,
   right 6.1/2.3 ms). 0 = mono.

7. out_gain_db — -24…+12 dB, default 0 dB
   Final output trim. Does not count toward the 8-param limit.

INLETS / OUTLETS
- in1 / in2: stereo audio; also the gate's sidechain (summed for the comb bank,
  the wet signal is rebuilt by the two allpass pairs).
- in3: connected but unused (batch inlet layout).
- in4: one-bar phasor. Only the bar *length* is used: it is measured
  wrap-to-wrap and held, so gate_div means "1/8 of a bar". With in4 static the
  last measured bar is kept, or 2.000 s (120 BPM) if in4 was never seen. A hit
  that lands before the first bar has completed uses that 120 BPM fallback —
  from the second bar on, the measured tempo is exact.
- out1 / out2: stereo wet only.

NOTES
- The trigger is an attack detector: a fast (~2 ms) envelope against a slow
  (~150 ms) one. Each new attack re-opens the gate; a legato sustain without a
  fresh articulation keeps it closed after its hold time.
- The detector is relative to the slow envelope with a small absolute floor
  (about -48 dBFS), so quiet playing still triggers it.
- Body decay is derived from the hold time (twice the gate), so a long gate is
  still full when it cuts; gate_shape is what makes it fade instead.
- Gate gain is smoothed (~1 ms) and the cut edge has a short release ramp, so
  the switch cannot click. The combs keep a tiny feedback leak while closed so
  they empty instead of bursting back.
- Comb lengths are 19.3, 22.7, 27.5, 33.9, 41.7 and 51.1 ms.
- No pitch tracking, no limiter, no dry path.
