JClones_DuTape — RNBO codebox~ port of JClones_DuTape (JSFX)
Source: repos/JSFXClones/jsfx/JClones_DuTape.jsfx (MIT, © JClones)

WHAT IT IS
A tape-saturation emulation: per-channel hiss, the machine's record/playback
tone EQ (one-pole treble loss, bass bump and head bump), a measured 256-point
transfer curve per machine, optional dither and a 3 Hz DC remover on the
asymmetric machines. Two independent channels, each with its own noise
generator, as in the source.

PARAMETERS
input_db — -12..+6 dB, default 0, linear taper. How hard the signal hits the
  tape curve. +3..+6 is where the curve starts to genuinely squash a tuba;
  negative values clean it up.

output_db — -12..+6 dB, default 0, linear taper. Makeup only.

machine — Ideal Tape / 80s 2 Tk. / 70s 24Tk. / 60s 2 Tk. / 60s Tube / 1/2 M.Tk.,
  default Ideal Tape. Changes the transfer curve, the hiss level and its EQ, and
  whether the DC remover runs (only 70s 24Tk. and later). Hiss at unity NR:
  Ideal Tape is nearly silent (‑63 dBFS-ish), 60s Tube is the noisiest, 1/2 M.Tk.
  has a bright/hissy top. "Ideal Tape" is the cleanest, most symmetric curve.

speed — 30 ips / 15 ips / 7 1/2 ips, default 30 ips. Scales the tape's
  record/playback response: 30 ips is the most extended and least coloured,
  15 ips is the classic middle, 7 1/2 ips rolls off the top and adds the biggest
  low-end bump. Stepped; switching mid-note is audible (that's the medium).

noise_reduction — Off / Type 1 / Type 2 / Noiseless, default Noiseless. Scales
  the hiss only: 100 % / 47 % / 22 % / 0 % of the machine's hiss. Does not change
  the transfer curve. Noiseless is the sensible default on a loud stage.

dither — Off / On, default On. The original plugin's internal dither: a tiny
  random gain applied to negative noise half-cycles (≈-0.2..-0.7 % depending on
  machine), which also slightly de-correlates L/R hiss. Purely a texture switch —
  leave it on unless you want identical, "cleaner" hiss.

NOTES
- No wet/dry: the tape stage is meant to be the sound. Put it late in the chain.
- The measured curves are stored in full (both polarities), so the asymmetric
  machines keep their even-order behaviour; the DC remover that follows them is
  part of the original and not optional.
- Filter coefficient tables were calibrated at 44.1 kHz in the source and are
  rescaled by 44100/samplerate here, so the response tracks your rate.
