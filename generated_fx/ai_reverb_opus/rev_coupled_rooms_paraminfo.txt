================================================================================
rev_coupled_rooms — Parameter Information
================================================================================

WHAT IT IS
An eight-line grouped feedback delay network: four short lines (11–20 ms) are
a small, tight room, and four long lines (61–103 ms, each with an allpass) are
a huge hall. A coupling angle opens an "aperture" between them, so the tail
drops fast (the small room), then turns into a slow, dark second slope (the
hall bleeding back). That is the double-slope decay of coupled spaces.

Source: Das, Schlecht, Abel et al., DAFx-20 Paper 25 §2 Eq. 1–3, §3.1 Eq. 4–6
(GFDN, per-group T60, coupled orthonormal mixing matrix); Cassidy et al.,
DAFx-24 Paper 73 §2 (double-slope decay).

The point of this one is punch and depth at once: the attack sits in a small
room and stays tight on the beat, while a distant hall swells in behind it.
100% wet, no dry/wet.

PARAMETERS (7 + output gain)

1. predelay_ms — 0…200 ms, default 5 ms
   Gap before both rooms.

2. small_decay_s — 0.1…2 s, default 0.4 s
   RT60 of the small room: the first, steep slope.

3. big_decay_s — 1…20 s, default 6 s
   RT60 of the hall: the second, slow slope.

4. coupling — 0…1, default 0.35
   Aperture between the rooms (coupling angle 0…pi/4). 0 = small room only;
   0.2–0.5 = clear double slope with the knee ~25 dB down; 1 = the rooms share
   their energy, so the hall gets louder but also decays faster.

5. diffuse — 0…1, default 1.0
   Mixing angle inside each room (0 = no mixing, separate echoes/flutter;
   1 = Hadamard, dense). Low values give a metallic, slapback-like small room.

6. damp_hz — 300…12000 Hz, default 2000 Hz
   Lowpass in the hall lines: how dark the second slope is. The small room is
   fixed at 9 kHz.

7. lowcut_hz — 20…600 Hz, default 100 Hz
   Two one-pole highpasses on the input. Raise it to keep the sub out of the
   hall.

8. out_gain_db — -24…+12 dB, default 0 dB
   Final output trim. Does not count toward the 8-param limit.

INLETS / OUTLETS
- in1 / in2: stereo audio (into the small room, and into the hall by
  sin(coupling angle)).
- out1 / out2: stereo wet only (even lines left, odd lines right).

NOTES
- The mixing matrix is orthonormal at every setting, so the network is stable;
  the decay comes only from the per-line gains and lowpasses.
- The hall lines wobble by +/-0.08 ms at 0.23 Hz so the long tail never rings.
- No limiter, no dry path.
