================================================================================
14_inst_metallic_comb_growler — Parameter Information
================================================================================

OVERVIEW
A signature modern riddim / tearout "metallic talking pipe" instrument. Combines three core technologies from the papers:
1. Delay-Based Asymmetric Adaptive FM (Lazzarini et al. DAFx-08) to inject moving upper-sideband formants onto every harmonic of the tuba.
2. Pitch-tracked cubic-interpolated stereo waveguide comb resonators swept by a kink-warped 1-bar phasor envelope.
3. A 6-stage Allpass Phase Disperser whose corner frequency tracks 0.75x the comb resonant pitch, turning metallic flange peaks into watery, dispersed dubstep laser vowels.

INLETS & OUTLETS
- in1 / in2: Stereo Tuba Audio Input (Left / Right; in2 silent for 250 ms -> in1 is used for both sides)
- in3: Tracked Fundamental Pitch in Hz (from fluid.pitch~; falls back to internal zero-crossing tracker if <= 0)
- in4: 1-Bar Audio-Rate Phasor [0..1] (syncs the rhythmic comb whip; an internal 0.5 Hz bar takes over when in4 has not moved for 100 ms - a held value no longer freezes the whip)
- out1 / out2: Stereo Metallic Comb Growler Output
- Level: on the sousa_mic.aif take (-32 dB RMS in) the defaults give about -31 dB RMS, -27 dB RMS at comb_feedback +0.98, and stay within about 3 dB across the Joy Y range (offline render of the real RNBO code). The comb loop has a 15 Hz DC blocker (without it, high feedback built up -0.05 of DC) and its delay is corrected for the loop filters' phase so the comb stays in tune.

PARAMETERS (8)

1. joy_x_comb_morph (Joystick X-Axis)
   - Range: -1.0 to +1.0 (Center-zero bipolar joystick axis)
   - Taper: Exponential octave offset (+/- 1.8 octaves around 2 * f0).
   - Function: Sweeps the pitch-tracked comb delay ratio from deep sub-octave hollow pipes (-1.0) through fundamental-locked metallic throat resonance (0.0) to high-pitched robotic screech pipes (+1.0).

2. joy_y_adfm_growl (Joystick Y-Axis)
   - Range: -1.0 to +1.0 (Center-zero bipolar joystick axis)
   - Taper: Power curve (x^1.3) on FM modulation index (0.4 to 4.6) paired with exponential Palamin symmetry factor r = 3.2^y. The asymmetric envelope is RMS-normalised (by sqrt(I0(2k))), so the level holds while you push the stick (peak normalisation used to cost 12 dB at +1).
   - Function: Controls the pre-comb Asymmetric AdFM stage. Pulling back (-1.0) shifts FM sidebands below the carrier for dark sub-grit; pushing forward (+1.0) blasts upper-sideband moving formants into the comb resonator.

3. whip_subdiv
   - Range: 1 to 16 cycles per bar (Quantized in 16 integer steps)
   - Taper: Stepped integer multiplier of the 1-bar phasor.
   - Function: Sets the rhythmic subdivision rate of the kink-warped comb pitch-whip envelope.

4. whip_depth
   - Range: 0.0 to 1.0 (maps to 0.0 to 2.4 octaves of rhythmic comb sweep)
   - Taper: Exponential octave depth.
   - Function: Determines how far each beat subdivision whips the comb resonator pitch upward and back down.

5. comb_feedback
   - Range: -0.98 to +0.98 (Bipolar Waveguide Feedback)
   - Taper: Reverse-power curve (1 - (1 - |x|)^1.4) for high precision near self-oscillation.
   - Function: Positive values produce bright, open-pipe metallic ring; negative values produce hollow, odd-harmonic stopped-pipe resonance.

6. comb_damping
   - Range: 0.0 to 1.0 (maps to loop lowpass cutoff from 450 Hz to 17,100 Hz)
   - Taper: Exponential frequency taper (450 * 38^x Hz).
   - Function: Adjusts high-frequency absorption inside the comb feedback loop from warm brassy wood to razor-sharp steel.

7. disperse_pinch
   - Range: 0.0 to 1.0 (maps to Allpass Q from 0.707 to 7.2 and wet blend 0% to 100%)
   - Taper: Power curve (x^1.4) on allpass Q.
   - Function: Simultaneously blends in the 6-stage allpass disperser and pinches its group-delay spike around the comb frequency.

8. stereo_width
   - Range: 0.0 to 1.0 (0.0 to +/- 0.35 octaves L/R comb detune)
   - Taper: Power curve (x^1.5) for fine micro-cent stereo widening in the lower half.
   - Function: Splits the Left and Right comb waveguide frequencies in opposite directions.
