================================================================================
12_adaa_chebyshev_wavefolder — Parameter Information
================================================================================

OVERVIEW
Combines First-Order Antiderivative Antialiased (ADAA1) Wavefolding (Bilbao, Esqueda, Parker, & Välimäki, IEEE SPL 2017) with Chebyshev Polynomial Harmonic Synthesis (Arfib 1979 / Airwindows Pafnuty2) and low-octave preservation (Focus / UnBox). Evaluating the analytical antiderivative F1(x) = -cos(pi*x)/pi + 0.5*ln(cosh(x)) over each sample interval reduces foldback aliasing without brute-force oversampling, while the Chebyshev T2–T5 stage injects mostly deterministic 2nd–5th harmonics. Neither path is alias-free: measured on a 1.2 kHz sine at 30 dB drive, the inharmonic (aliased) energy is about 12 dB below the harmonics for the folder and 27 dB below for the Chebyshev side (it follows a soft tanh limiter). On the tuba's low notes this is far less.

INLETS & OUTLETS
- in1 / in2: Stereo Audio Input (works great on both raw tuba and 01_harmonic_isolator)
- out1 / out2: Stereo Antialiased Wavefolded / Chebyshev Output
- No in3 / in4 (no pitch or tempo input).
- Level: on the sousa_mic.aif take (-32 dB RMS in) the defaults give about -20 / -17.5 dB RMS (L / R; the bias has opposite polarity per side) with peaks around -6 / -2.5 dBFS (offline render of the real RNBO code). Silence stays silent: the output at the bias point is removed before the DC blocker and the bias glides over 10 ms, so loading the effect or moving asym_bias no longer clicks (it used to spike to -0.6 dBFS on load).

PARAMETERS (4)

1. drive_db
   - Range: 0.0 to 30.0 dB
   - Taper: Logarithmic dB-to-linear amplitude (dbtoa(drive_db), 1.0x to 31.6x gain).
   - Function: Drives the tuba waveform through multiple sinusoidal wavefolds or saturates the Chebyshev harmonic polynomials.

2. fold_cheby
   - Range: 0.0 to 1.0 (0.0 = ADAA1 West-Coast Wavefolder, 1.0 = Chebyshev T2–T5 Harmonic Generator)
   - Taper: Linear crossfade.
   - Function: Morphs between metallic, formant-sweeping West-Coast wavefolding (0.0) and controlled, zero-aliasing Chebyshev polynomial overtone synthesis (1.0).

3. asym_bias
   - Range: -0.60 to +0.60 (DC Offset / Single-Ended Triode Asymmetry)
   - Taper: Linear bipolar offset (applied in opposite polarity on Left and Right channels for stereo harmonic spread; glides over 10 ms, and the resulting static offset is subtracted before a 15 Hz DC blocker).
   - Function: Shifts the operating point of the folder/Chebyshev curve to inject warm even-order harmonics (2nd, 4th) that fatten the tuba's odd-dominant natural spectrum.

4. sub_preserve
   - Range: 0.0 to 1.0 (0% to 100% Clean Sub-95 Hz Fundamental Reinjection)
   - Taper: Power curve (x^1.2).
   - Function: Splits off the clean sub-95 Hz fundamental via a one-pole lowpass before distortion and sums it back post-clipper so extreme high-drive folding never loses sub-bass weight.
