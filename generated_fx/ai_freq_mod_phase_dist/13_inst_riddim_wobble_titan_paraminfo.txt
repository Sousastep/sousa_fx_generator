================================================================================
13_inst_riddim_wobble_titan — Parameter Information
================================================================================

OVERVIEW
A complete live tuba-to-dubstep/riddim synthesizer instrument designed for thumb-joystick performance. Internally isolates the tuba's fundamental via a pitch-tracked peaking/SVF front end, normalizes it into a phase-locked modulator, drives an Adaptive FM + Sub-Octave oscillator core through an ADAA1 wavefolder and an 8-stage Allpass Phase Disperser, and sculpts the result through a 1-bar-phasor-synced dual state-variable wobble filter.

INLETS & OUTLETS
- in1 / in2: Stereo Tuba Audio Input (Left / Right; in2 silent for 250 ms -> in1 is used for both sides)
- in3: Tracked Fundamental Pitch in Hz (from fluid.pitch~; falls back to internal zero-crossing tracker if <= 0)
- in4: 1-Bar Audio-Rate Phasor [0..1] (syncs the wobble rhythm; falls back to an internal 0.5 Hz bar when in4 has not moved for 100 ms - a held value no longer freezes the wobble)
- out1 / out2: Stereo Riddim Wobble Instrument Output
- Level & dynamics: the synth level and the dynamic part of the FM index follow how hard you play (the fundamental isolator is now linear; it used to saturate, so the output sat at a +/-1 square wave whatever the dynamics). A final tanh soft-limits, trimmed by the filter resonance. On the sousa_mic.aif take (-32 dB RMS in) the defaults give about -10.5 dB RMS with about 5% of samples in the soft limiter; 20 dB softer playing gives about -29 dB RMS (offline render of the real RNBO code).
- The sub-octave is pitch-locked (a free phasor at f0 / 2), not phase-locked to the tuba.

PARAMETERS (8)

1. joy_x_filtsweep (Joystick X-Axis)
   - Range: -1.0 to +1.0 (Center-zero bipolar joystick axis)
   - Taper: Exponential octave mapping (70 * 90^x Hz, spanning 70 Hz sub-growl to 6,300 Hz open roar).
   - Function: Manual offset/override on the dual-SVF wobble filter cutoff frequency for expressive thumb-controlled filter sweeps and fills.

2. joy_y_fm_scream (Joystick Y-Axis)
   - Range: -1.0 to +1.0 (Center-zero bipolar joystick axis)
   - Taper: Power curve (x^1.3) on FM index (1.2 to 6.7), ADAA wavefolder drive, bandpass formant emphasis, and upper harmonic carrier ratio steps (carrier jumps to 2x f0 at +0.5, 3x at +0.75 and 4x at +1.0).
   - Function: Pushing the joystick forward transforms a deep chesty sub-wobble into a screaming tearout/riddim harmonic screech ("yoink").

3. wobble_rate
   - Range: 1 to 16 cycles per bar (Quantized in 16 integer steps)
   - Taper: Stepped integer multiplier of the 1-bar phasor (1 = whole bar, 2 = half, 3 = half-triplet, 4 = quarter, 6 = quarter-triplet, 8 = eighth, 12 = eighth-triplet, 16 = sixteenth).
   - Function: Sets the rhythmic subdivision of the synced wobble LFO.

4. wobble_shape
   - Range: 0.0 to 1.0
   - Taper: Three-shape continuous morph:
     * 0.00: Smooth raised-cosine dubstep wobble
     * 0.50: Asymmetric fast-attack / curved-decay riddim whip (duty = 0.20)
     * 1.00: Gated square-ramp riddim chop (hard silence gap between chops)
   - Function: Shapes the contour of each wobble cycle.

5. sine_boost_db
   - Range: 0.0 to 36.0 dB
   - Taper: Logarithmic dB scale (10^(dB/40)) with automatic gain compensation.
   - Function: Sets the peaking EQ boost at f0 before the modulator normalizer (after the automatic compensation the net gain at f0 is 0.375 * boost_db, i.e. 9 dB at the 24 dB default). Higher values yield cleaner, more synthetic FM sidebands; lower values let raw brass overtones grit up the FM carrier.

6. sub_oct_level
   - Range: 0.0 to 1.0 (0% to 100% Sub-Octave Foundation)
   - Taper: Linear amplitude (includes a 1st + 3rd harmonic sub-waveform for chest-hitting weight on PA systems).
   - Function: Blends in a pitch-locked f0/2 sub-octave oscillator before the wobble filter.

7. disperse_amt
   - Range: 0.0 to 1.0 (0% Dry to 100% 8-Stage Allpass Dispersion)
   - Taper: Linear crossfade.
   - Function: Routes the pre-filter distorted bass through an 8-stage allpass cascade tuned to 2.5 * f0, smearing each wobble transient into a liquid laser punch.

8. filter_res
   - Range: 0.0 to 1.0 (maps to the svf resonance setting 0.55 .. 0.95, i.e. a filter Q of about 3.4 .. 280; 21 at the 0.72 default)
   - Taper: Power curve (x^1.2).
   - Function: Controls the vocal resonance peak of the dual-stage morphing wobble filter.
