================================================================================
10_phasor_synced_comb_flanger — Parameter Information
================================================================================

OVERVIEW
A tempo-synced stereo comb/flange sweeper driven by the 1-bar audio-rate phasor (0 to 1) on in4. Multiplies and wraps the 1-bar phasor into integer rhythmic subdivisions (quarter notes, 8ths, triplets, 16ths) and shapes each cycle into an asymmetric fast-rise / curved-decay riddim whip envelope. Sweeps a pair of cubic-interpolated resonant comb filters across up to 3.5 octaves to turn steady tuba notes into syncopated, metallic riddim chop-sweeps.

INLETS & OUTLETS
- in1 / in2: Stereo Audio Input (Left / Right)
- in3: Tracked pitch in Hz (rig contract; not used by this effect)
- in4: 1-Bar Audio-Rate Phasor [0..1] (falls back to an internal 0.5 Hz LFO when in4 has not moved for 100 ms). Earlier versions read the bar from in3, so in the rig the sweep followed the fractional part of the pitch instead of the bar.
- out1 / out2: Stereo Rhythmic Comb-Swept Audio Output

PARAMETERS (4)

1. subdiv_rate
   - Range: 1 to 16 cycles per bar (Quantized in 16 integer steps)
   - Taper: Stepped integer multiplier of the 1-bar phasor (1 = 1 bar, 2 = half notes, 3 = half-note triplets, 4 = quarter notes, 6 = quarter triplets, 8 = eighth notes, 12 = eighth triplets, 16 = sixteenth notes).
   - Function: Sets the rhythmic rate of the comb sweep within the 1-bar phasor window.

2. base_ms
   - Range: 0.15 to 15.0 ms (equivalent to comb base frequencies from ~66 Hz to 6,666 Hz)
   - Taper: Direct delay time in milliseconds (exponentially modulated in octaves by the sweep envelope).
   - Function: Sets the center delay time of the stereo comb filters. Short values (0.3–2.0 ms) create screaming jet-flange vowels; longer values (3.0–12.0 ms) create hollow metallic tube whips.

3. sweep_depth
   - Range: 0.0 to 1.0 (maps to 0.0 to 3.5 octaves of delay modulation)
   - Taper: Power curve (x^1.3) into exponential octave scaling (2^(env * 3.5)).
   - Function: Controls how far the rhythmic envelope sweeps the comb delay time on each beat subdivision.

4. resonance
   - Range: -0.96 to +0.96 (Bipolar Comb Feedback/Feedforward Gain)
   - Taper: Linear bipolar feedback with internal tanh soft-clipping.
   - Function: Positive values produce bright harmonic flange peaks; negative values produce hollow, odd-harmonic phase-inverted comb sweeps.
