================================================================================
18_inst_harmonic_cannon_polybass — Parameter Information
================================================================================

OVERVIEW
Implements the Pitch-Tracked Multiband Adaptive Processor (Approach 6 from "Novel Distortion Approaches for Live Tuba") combined with Airwindows Focus/BassDrive philosophy. Because the tuba's fundamental f0 is known, the crossover frequencies slide dynamically with the played pitch at 1.5 * f0 and 3.5 * f0 so each band always contains a fixed set of harmonics across the entire compass of the horn:
- Band 1 (< 1.5 * f0, Fundamental): Kept pristine and reinforced with a zero-crossing-locked sub-octave wave. (The crossovers are gentle 12 dB/oct state-variable filters, so neighbouring harmonics bleed between bands.)
- Band 2 (1.5 * f0 to 3.5 * f0, 2nd & 3rd Harmonics): Processed through ICASSP 2009 Coefficient-Modulated Allpass Phase Distortion and Chebyshev T2/T3/T5 polynomial waveshaping.
- Band 3 (> 3.5 * f0, Upper Brass Overtones): Routed into a stereo pitch-tracked metallic comb resonator, then sculpted by dual vowel formant filters (F1 & F2).

INLETS & OUTLETS
- in1 / in2: Stereo Tuba Audio Input (Left / Right; in2 silent for 250 ms -> in1 is used for both sides)
- in3: Tracked Fundamental Pitch in Hz (from fluid.pitch~; falls back to internal zero-crossing tracker if <= 0)
- in4: 1-Bar Audio-Rate Phasor [0..1] (syncs multiband comb and vowel formant motion; an internal 0.5 Hz bar takes over when in4 has not moved for 100 ms - a held value no longer freezes the motion)
- out1 / out2: Stereo Multiband Harmonic Cannon Output
- Level: on the sousa_mic.aif take (-32 dB RMS in) the defaults give about -14 / -16 dB RMS (L / R) with about 1.5% of samples in the final tanh limiter (offline render of the real RNBO code). Band 2 now has a DC blocker and silence stays silent: its Chebyshev T2 term used to put about +/-0.09 of DC on the outputs at all times.

PARAMETERS (8)

1. joy_x_vowel_tilt (Joystick X-Axis)
   - Range: -1.0 to +1.0 (Center-zero bipolar joystick axis)
   - Taper: Exponential formant frequency mapping (F1 sweeps 240–910 Hz; F2 counter-sweeps 750–2,625 Hz; Band 3 comb shifts +/- 0.8 octaves).
   - Function: Morphs the mid/high harmonic bands across vocal dubstep formants ("oo" -> "ah" -> "ee"/"yoink") while leaving Band 1's sub-fundamental untouched.

2. joy_y_multidrive (Joystick Y-Axis)
   - Range: -1.0 to +1.0 (Center-zero bipolar joystick axis)
   - Taper: Linear-to-exponential drive scaling across Band 2 Chebyshev saturation, Allpass PD depth, Band 3 comb excitation, and F2 formant brightness.
   - Function: Acts as a master "aggression / bloom" axis for the upper two harmonic bands while Band 1 maintains rock-solid low-end weight.

3. sub_foundation
   - Range: 0.0 to 1.0 (0% to 100% Locked Sub-Octave Injection in Band 1)
   - Taper: Power curve (x^1.2).
   - Function: Controls the level of the f0-locked sub-octave generator summed with the isolated fundamental in Band 1. Its flip-flop and envelope come from a dedicated bandpass at f0 (band 1 alone still carries the tuba's 2nd harmonic, which made the old flip-flop toggle at 2 f0 so no sub-octave came out).

4. mid_allpass_pd
   - Range: 0.0 to 1.0 (0% to 100% Coefficient-Modulated Allpass Phase Distortion on Band 2)
   - Taper: Linear modulation of the ICASSP 2009 tanh-smoothed sawtooth allpass coefficient m(t) in [0.15, 0.93].
   - Function: Imparts sawtooth phase distortion specifically to the 2nd and 3rd harmonics of the tuba.

5. high_comb_metal
   - Range: 0.0 to 1.0 (maps to Band 3 comb feedback from 0.0 to 0.88)
   - Taper: Linear feedback scaling with internal tanh loop saturation.
   - Function: Controls the metallic pipe resonance applied exclusively to harmonics above 3.5 * f0.

6. motion_subdiv
   - Range: 1 to 16 cycles per bar (Quantized in 16 integer steps)
   - Taper: Stepped integer multiplier of the 1-bar phasor.
   - Function: Sets the rhythmic subdivision rate of the synced formant and comb modulation LFO.

7. motion_depth
   - Range: 0.0 to 1.0
   - Taper: Asymmetric triangle LFO (duty = 0.30) modulating both the Band 3 comb pitch (up to +1 octave) and the F1/F2 vowel formant cutoffs.
   - Function: Controls the depth of the 1-bar synced rhythmic animation.

8. stereo_spread
   - Range: 0.0 to 1.0 (maps to +/- 25% L/R comb delay offset and +/- 10% vowel formant offset)
   - Taper: Linear stereo divergence (Band 1 fundamental and sub-octave remain 100% mono-centered for club/PA sub compatibility).
   - Function: Widens the stereo image of the upper two harmonic bands while keeping the sub-bass mono.
