================================================================================
16_inst_phaseshaper_matrix_lead — Parameter Information
================================================================================

OVERVIEW
A full phaseshaping synthesizer instrument driven by the tuba's analytic phase phi(t), uniting all four oscillator algorithms from Kleimola, Lazzarini, Timoney, and Välimäki (SMC 2010):
- Voice 1 (Joystick Y Down): Variable-Slope Parabolic Triangle Phase gvtri -> Sinusoid (Eq. 16–17), producing vocal formant peaks where every 4th harmonic outside the formant is suppressed.
- Voice 2 (Joystick Y Center): PolyBLEP Single-Oscillator Hard Sync (Eq. 22) layered with Roland JP-8000 Triangle Modulation (Eq. 26).
- Voice 3 (Joystick Y Up): Roland JP-8000 Ripple Supersaw (Eq. 28) with quadrature stereo m1 modulation.
Includes a phase-unwrapped sub-octave foundation cos(theta/2) so every voice stays anchored to massive dubstep sub-bass.

INLETS & OUTLETS
- in1 / in2: Stereo Tuba Audio Input (Left / Right; in2 silent for 250 ms -> in1 is used for both sides)
- in3: Tracked Fundamental Pitch in Hz (from fluid.pitch~; falls back to internal zero-crossing tracker if <= 0)
- in4: 1-Bar Audio-Rate Phasor [0..1] (drives rhythmic sync-ratio sweeps and supersaw LFO; an internal 0.5 Hz bar takes over when in4 has not moved for 100 ms - a held value no longer freezes them)
- out1 / out2: Stereo Phaseshaper Matrix Instrument Output
- Level & dynamics: follows the tuba through a 15 ms envelope follower, then a 15 Hz DC blocker and a tanh soft limiter. On the sousa_mic.aif take (-32 dB RMS in) the defaults give about -21 dB RMS / -5 dBFS peak, and -17..-21 dB RMS across the Joy Y, pre_sine_iso and sub_lock_mix range (offline render of the real RNBO code). Earlier versions took the level from the isolation bandpass (about 45x gain), which pinned the output at the limiter and made pre_sine_iso an 18 dB volume knob; Voice 2's Triangle Modulation also carried a -0.9 DC term that thumped with every note (now centred).

PARAMETERS (8)

1. joy_x_sync_sweep (Joystick X-Axis)
   - Range: -1.0 to +1.0 (Center-zero bipolar joystick axis)
   - Taper: Exponential ratio taper (2.5 * 3.2^x, spanning 0.78x Minimoog waveslice at -1.0 to 8.0x screaming hard-sync formant at +1.0).
   - Function: Sweeps the master fractional-period multiplier a1 across all three phaseshaper engines simultaneously.

2. joy_y_morph_algo (Joystick Y-Axis)
   - Range: -1.0 to +1.0 (Center-zero bipolar joystick axis)
   - Taper: Three-point continuous crossfade (-1.0 = Tilted-Triangle Sinusoidal Formant, 0.0 = PolyBLEP Hard Sync + JP-8000 Triangle Mod, +1.0 = Ripple Supersaw Unison).
   - Function: Morphs the phaseshaping topology in real time while playing.

3. tilt_duty_w
   - Range: 0.10 to 0.90 (Duty Width w of Parabolic Triangle Phase svtri, Eq. 16)
   - Taper: Linear duty cycle centered at 0.50.
   - Function: Tilts the up-ramp vs. down-ramp slope of the extracted phase cycle, shifting the spectral tilt and hollow formant notches.

4. supersaw_ripple
   - Range: 0.0 to 1.0 (maps to ripple modulus m1 in [0.20, 0.75] with m2 = 0.88)
   - Taper: Linear ripple depth.
   - Function: Sets the detuned sawtooth density and stereo chorus spread of the Voice 3 Ripple Supersaw generator.

5. rhythm_subdiv
   - Range: 1 to 16 cycles per bar (Quantized in 16 integer steps)
   - Taper: Stepped integer multiplier of the 1-bar phasor.
   - Function: Sets the rhythmic subdivision rate of the synced phase-ratio envelope.

6. rhythm_depth
   - Range: 0.0 to 1.0 (adds 0.0 to +5.0 to the sync ratio a1 on each beat)
   - Taper: Asymmetric fast-attack triangle envelope (duty = 0.20, power 1.5).
   - Function: Whips the phaseshaper sync ratio a1 rhythmically in sync with the 1-bar phasor.

7. sub_lock_mix
   - Range: 0.0 to 1.0 (0% to 100% Phase-Divided Sub-Octave cos(theta / 2))
   - Taper: Linear blend with automatic headroom compensation on the upper lead voices.
   - Function: Mixes in the zero-drift unwrapped sub-octave sine wave underneath the phaseshaped lead.

8. pre_sine_iso
   - Range: 0.0 to 1.0 (0% Raw Tuba into Hilbert to 100% SVF-Isolated Fundamental into Hilbert)
   - Taper: Linear crossfade with Q scaling.
   - Function: Controls the purity of the analytic phasor phi(t). At 1.0, phi(t) is a glass-smooth ramp; lower values let residual brass overtones inject organic phase grain. It no longer changes the output level.
