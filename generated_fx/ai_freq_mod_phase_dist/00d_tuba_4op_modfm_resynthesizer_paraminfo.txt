================================================================================
00d_tuba_4op_modfm_resynthesizer — Parameter Information
================================================================================

OVERVIEW
A 4-Operator Adaptive ModFM & Phaseshaper Vocoder Resynthesizer designed to be patched directly 1-to-1 from the 9 outlets of `00c_tuba_4harmonic_ift_analyzer.txt` (plus an optional 1-bar phasor on `in10`).
Implements the ModFM Synthesis Vocoder concept from Lazzarini & Timoney (ICMC 2011 / DAFx-08) combined with SMC 2010 PolyBLEP Hard-Sync Phaseshaping:
- Each of the 4 tracked tuba harmonics (f1..f4, A1..A4) drives its own ModFM operator s_k(t) = A_k(t) * carrier_k(phi_k) * exp(k_op * (cos(2*pi*phi_mod) - 1)), generating strictly monotonic Modified Bessel sidebands around each partial that never cancel or clip.
- The tuba's real-time Spectral Centroid (`in9`) automatically couples your acoustic brass brightness (soft dark tone vs. brassy fortissimo) to the ModFM index and sync formant ratio.
- Measures the real-time inharmonic deviation of partials 2, 3, and 4 from integer multiples of f1 and lets you scale it from pure harmonic lock (0.0) up to 4x exaggerated ghost-note metallic clangor.

INLETS & OUTLETS
- in1..in4: Harmonics 1..4 Instantaneous Frequencies f1, f2, f3, f4 in Hz (from out1..out4 of 00c)
- in5..in8: Harmonics 1..4 Instantaneous Amplitudes A1, A2, A3, A4 (from out5..out8 of 00c)
- in9: Real-Time Brass Spectral Centroid Ratio [1.0 to 4.0] (from out9 of 00c)
- in10: Optional 1-Bar Audio-Rate Phasor [0.0 to 1.0] (syncs rhythmic ModFM/Sync wobble; falls back to an internal 0.5 Hz / 2 s bar when in10 has not moved for 100 ms)
- out1 / out2: Stereo 4-Operator ModFM Resynthesizer Output
- Level: follows the A1..A4 inputs. Fed from 00c on the sousa_mic.aif take (input -32 dB RMS) the output measures about -36 dB RMS at the defaults (offline render of the real RNBO code); a final tanh soft-clips anything hot.

PARAMETERS (8)

1. joy_x_sync_formant (Joystick X-Axis)
   - Range: -1.0 to +1.0 (Center-zero bipolar joystick axis)
   - Taper: Exponential sync ratio 1.8 * 2.6^x (clamped to 1.0x below about x = -0.6; 4.7x at +1.0, up to about 7.5x when bright playing and the wobble push it) coupled with a sinusoid-to-PolyBLEP-sawtooth carrier morph (sawtooth share 0..75%).
   - Function: Sweeps the internal hard-sync formant ratio of all 4 harmonic operators simultaneously.

2. joy_y_modfm_index (Joystick Y-Axis)
   - Range: -1.0 to +1.0 (Center-zero bipolar joystick axis)
   - Taper: Power curve (x^1.35) scaling the ModFM exponential bandwidth index k_op from about 0.1 (dark, soft playing) up to 12.0 (clamped), while progressively boosting operators 2, 3, and 4.
   - Function: Expands each of the 4 tuba partials from a narrow harmonic into a wide, growling Modified-Bessel formant cluster.

3. inharm_stretch
   - Range: 0.0 to 4.0 (Default: 1.0)
   - Taper: Linear multiplier on each partial's measured deviation (f_k - k * f_1):
     * 0.0: Forces all 4 operators into strict integer harmonic lock (1x, 2x, 3x, 4x f1)
     * 1.0: Preserves the exact natural bore micro-inharmonicity of your tuba
     * 1.0 to 4.0: Exaggerates ghost-note pull and wave-steepening deviations into metallic dubstep bell/tearout sidebands
   - Function: Scales the physical inharmonicity measured by the 4-harmonic IFT analyzer (00c).

4. cross_op_fm
   - Range: 0.0 to 1.0 (Default: 0.35)
   - Taper: Power curve (x^1.3) scaling inter-operator phase modulation depth from 0.0 to 0.45 cycles (about 2.8 radians).
   - Function: Routes the output of Operator k-1 to phase-modulate Operator k (with Operator 4 feeding back into Operator 1), turning the 4 additive ModFM operators into a circular 4-op FM synthesis matrix.

5. sub_octave_mix
   - Range: 0.0 to 1.0 (Default: 0.65)
   - Taper: Linear level scaling weighted dynamically by Fundamental Amplitude A1 (in5).
   - Function: Injects a phase-locked 0.5 * f1 sub-octave oscillator (enriched with its 3rd harmonic) that tracks the exact volume envelope of your fundamental partial.

6. rhythm_subdiv
   - Range: 1 to 16 cycles per bar (Quantized in 16 integer steps)
   - Taper: Stepped integer multiplier of the 1-bar phasor on in10.
   - Function: Sets the rhythmic subdivision rate of the synced ModFM & Hard-Sync wobble envelope.

7. rhythm_wobble
   - Range: 0.0 to 1.0 (0% Continuous Breath Control to 100% Tempo-Synced Wobble Whip)
   - Taper: Linear depth modulating an asymmetric fast-attack triangle envelope (duty = 0.22, power = 1.5).
   - Function: Pulsates both the ModFM formant bandwidth and the Hard-Sync ratio in lockstep with the 1-bar phasor.

8. stereo_pan_spread
   - Range: 0.0 to 1.0 (Default: 0.60)
   - Taper: Constant-power cosine/sine stereo law plus opposite-polarity L/R cross-FM phase offset.
   - Function: Keeps Operator 1 (fundamental) and the Sub-Octave anchored dead-center in mono while fanning Operators 2, 3, and 4 progressively wider across the Left and Right channels.
