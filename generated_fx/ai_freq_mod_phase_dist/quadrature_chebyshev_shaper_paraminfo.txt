================================================================================
quadrature_chebyshev_shaper — Parameter Information
================================================================================

OVERVIEW
Implements Phase-Quadrature Chebyshev Waveshaping using Chebyshev Polynomials of the First Kind (T_k) and Second Kind (U_k) from Timoney, Lazzarini, Gibney, and Pekonen (DAFx-10 Paper P65, Section 2.1, Eq. 5–15). Standard Chebyshev waveshaping (`12_adaa_chebyshev_wavefolder`) uses only First-Kind polynomials T_k(cos(wt)) = cos(k*wt), locking every generated harmonic to 0-degree cosine phase. By extracting the quadrature signal pair (x_I = cos(wt), x_Q = sin(wt)) via a 4-stage Hilbert transform and combining T_k(x_I) with x_Q * U_{k-1}(x_I) = sin(k*wt) (Eq. 15), this effect gives you independent control over BOTH the harmonic magnitudes AND the exact phase angles phi_k of harmonics 2 through 6 — allowing you to tilt waveform asymmetry, emulate reactive analog distortion circuits, and continuously spin harmonic phases in opposite stereo directions! The quadrature pair is normalised onto the unit circle (where T_k and U_k are exact) and the level is restored afterwards, so the harmonic recipe is the same whether you play softly or loudly.

INLETS & OUTLETS
- in1 / in2: Stereo Audio Input (works best after 01_harmonic_isolator or on raw tuba; in2 silent for 250 ms -> in1 is used for both sides)
- in3: Optional Modulation Envelope [-2..2] (dynamically rotates harmonic phase angles phi_k on attacks; values outside that range, such as the rig's pitch in Hz, are ignored)
- in4: Optional 1-Bar Audio-Rate Phasor [0..1] (drives continuous harmonic phase rotation when phase_spin > 0)
- out1 / out2: Stereo Phase-Quadrature Chebyshev Output
- Level & behaviour (offline render of the real RNBO code): on a sine the defaults give a 2nd harmonic about 10 dB and a 3rd about 7 dB below the fundamental at 0.05, 0.3 and 0.9 peak alike. Earlier versions fed the polynomials the raw (sub-unit) magnitude, so at real tuba levels they returned almost only the fundamental (3rd harmonic 35 dB down at 0.05 peak). On the sousa_mic.aif take (-32 dB RMS in) the output is about -35 dB RMS.

PARAMETERS (7)

1. drive_db
   - Range: 0.0 to 18.0 dB (Default: 6.0 dB)
   - Taper: Logarithmic dB-to-linear gain on the envelope-normalised magnitude, through a tanh to a shaping radius r in [0, 1).
   - Function: Controls how strongly harmonics 2..6 come through: harmonic k is weighted by r^(k-1), so low drive keeps mostly the 2nd/3rd and high drive opens up the 5th/6th. Independent of playing level.

2. even_harmonics
   - Range: 0.0 to 1.0 (2nd, 4th, and 6th Harmonic Magnitude Weight, Default: 0.55)
   - Taper: Power curve (x^1.2).
   - Function: Controls the amplitude of the even Chebyshev harmonic pairs (T2/U1, T4/U3, T6/U5).

3. odd_harmonics
   - Range: 0.0 to 1.0 (3rd and 5th Harmonic Magnitude Weight, Default: 0.75)
   - Taper: Power curve (x^1.2).
   - Function: Controls the amplitude of the odd Chebyshev harmonic pairs (T3/U2, T5/U4).

4. high_order_tilt
   - Range: 0.0 to 1.0 (Low-Order T2/T3 vs. High-Order T4/T5/T6 Spectral Tilt, Default: 0.50)
   - Taper: Linear spectral tilt.
   - Function: Shifts harmonic weight from warm 2nd/3rd harmonics (0.0) toward aggressive upper 4th/5th/6th harmonics (1.0).

5. quad_phase_angle
   - Range: -3.14159 to +3.14159 rad (-180 to +180 degrees Harmonic Phase Angle phi, Default: +0.785 rad = 45 deg)
   - Taper: Linear angle in radians (DAFx-10 Eq. 10–15).
   - Function: Sets the progressive harmonic phase offset phi_k = k * phi between in-phase T_k (cosine) and quadrature x_Q*U_{k-1} (sine) polynomials, morphing the time-domain waveshape from symmetric peaky cosine (0 rad) to sawtooth/shock-tilted quadrature (+pi/2 rad) without changing the power spectrum!

6. phase_spin
   - Range: 0.0 to 1.0 (Continuous Harmonic Phase Rotation Depth, Default: 0.35)
   - Taper: Linear rotation rate locked to the 1-bar phasor on in4: phase_spin turns per half bar for harmonic 2, scaled by 1x..5x for harmonics 2..6 (an internal 0.5 Hz bar takes over when in4 has not moved for 100 ms).
   - Function: Continuously rotates each harmonic's phase angle, creating a zero-delay harmonic barberpole swirl inside the waveshaper. The rotation is integrated, so it never jumps at the bar wrap (earlier versions clicked twice per bar at fractional settings).

7. stereo_quad_spread
   - Range: 0.0 to 1.0 (maps to 0 to 90 degrees Left/Right Quadrature Phase Offset plus Counter-Rotation)
   - Taper: Linear stereo phase spread.
   - Function: Places Left and Right generated overtones in quadrature for an enveloping 3D stereo bass image that collapses cleanly in mono on the fundamental T1.
