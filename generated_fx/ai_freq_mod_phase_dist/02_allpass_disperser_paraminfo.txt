================================================================================
02_allpass_disperser — Parameter Information
================================================================================

OVERVIEW
A pure feed-forward cascade of up to 32 second-order biquad allpass filters per channel inspired by Kilohearts Disperser ("Disburser"). Because allpass filters have a flat magnitude response (0 dB across all frequencies) but a peaked group-delay curve around their corner frequency, stacking 32 stages multiplies the phase dispersion without carving phaser notches. Brass articulations and distorted harmonic spikes are unfolded in time into squelchy, liquid "chirp" transients.

INLETS & OUTLETS
- in1 / in2: Stereo Audio Input (Left / Right)
- in3: Optional Tracked Pitch in Hz (when 20..1000 Hz, the corner becomes freq_hz * pitch / 110, so the smear tracks the tuba's register; with the rig's tracker always patched, a 58 Hz Bb1 puts the default 180 Hz corner at about 95 Hz)
- no in4 (no tempo input)
- out1 / out2: Stereo Phase-Dispersed Audio Output

PARAMETERS (4)

1. stages
   - Range: 1.0 to 32.0 stages per channel
   - Taper: Rounded to whole stages; the count glides there over about 30 ms with an N / N+1 crossfade so moving it never clicks. (A held fractional count would be a 50% phaser mix that notches freq_hz by 15-19 dB, so it is not allowed to rest between stages.)
   - Function: Controls the total accumulated group delay (tau_total = N * tau_single). Low counts tighten sub punch; 16–32 stages create dramatic laser-like transient chirps.

2. freq_hz
   - Range: 30.0 to 4000.0 Hz
   - Taper: Direct value in Hz (smoothly slewed over 5 ms). Low settings (50–180 Hz) smear and fatten the tuba's sub-bass body; mid-high settings (300–2500 Hz) turn distorted brass buzz into liquid dubstep squelches.
   - Function: Sets the corner frequency of every allpass stage where maximum group delay occurs.

3. pinch_q
   - Range: 0.707 to 12.0 (Q / Resonance)
   - Taper: Direct Q factor (0.707 = Butterworth maximally flat delay bump; > 3.0 = tall, narrow resonant group-delay spike).
   - Function: Controls the sharpness ("Smash" / "Pinch") of the group delay peak around freq_hz. Higher values cause the time-smear to ring with a pitched, vocal-like metallic edge.

4. spread
   - Range: 0.0 to 1.0 (0 to +/- 0.75 octaves L/R offset)
   - Taper: Exponential octave offset (2^(spread * 0.75)).
   - Function: Offsets the Left and Right allpass cascade corner frequencies in opposite directions around freq_hz, creating massive stereo phase width while preserving mono magnitude compatibility.
