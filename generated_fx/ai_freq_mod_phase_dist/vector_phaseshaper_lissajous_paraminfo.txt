================================================================================
vector_phaseshaper_lissajous — Parameter Information
================================================================================

OVERVIEW
Implements 2D Vector Phaseshaping (VPS) Synthesis with Lissajous Vector Modulation and Incomplete-Period Alias Suppression from Kleimola, Lazzarini, Timoney, and Välimäki (DAFx-11 Paper 55_e, Sections 3, 3.1, 3.2, 4.3 & 5.2, Eq. 5–12, 20). Extracts the analytic phase phi(t) in [0, 1) from the tuba and maps it through a 2D inflection vector p = (d, v). While d controls horizontal pulse-width and brightness, v controls the vertical phase-wrap count, synthesizing sharp vocal formant peaks at f_formant = (2v - 1) * f0. Includes a 2D Lissajous modulator that traces circular, figure-eight, or multi-ring paths in (d, v) space at either tempo-synced LFO rates or pitch-locked audio rates.

INLETS & OUTLETS
- in1 / in2: Stereo Audio Input (prefer sine-ified tuba from 01_harmonic_isolator or raw tuba; in2 silent for 250 ms -> in1 is used for both sides)
- in3: Tracked Fundamental Pitch f0 in Hz (from tuba_freq_tracker_v2; falls back to internal zero-crossing tracker if <= 0)
- in4: Optional 1-Bar Audio-Rate Phasor [0..1] (syncs Lissajous LFO motion; when it stops moving for 100 ms the LFO carries on at 0.75 Hz from where the bar left it)
- in5: Optional Phase-Locked Tuba Phasor [0..1) (tuba_freq_tracker_v2 out3; the rig's s~ in5). While it moves, the shaped phase follows it exactly. Unpatched/static for 100 ms -> internal PLL (linear bandpass + Hilbert), continued from the last phase.
- in6: Optional Smoothed Tuba Envelope [0..1] (e.g. cascade_env_follower; the rig's s~ in6). Unpatched/static for 250 ms -> internal 4-stage cascaded follower (5 ms attack / 120 ms release).
- out1 / out2: Stereo Vector-Phaseshaped Formant Output
- Level: on the sousa_mic.aif take (-32 dB RMS in) about -20 dB RMS / -10.6 dBFS peak at the defaults, with or without in5/in6 (offline render of the real RNBO code). The DC blocker sits at 10 Hz so pedal-note fundamentals pass.

PARAMETERS (7)

1. vec_d
   - Range: 0.05 to 0.95 (Horizontal Inflection Breakpoint d, Default: 0.50)
   - Taper: Linear duty cycle centered at 0.50 (symmetric formant). Moving toward 0.05 or 0.95 spreads the formant into a bright sawtooth/pulse-like spectrum (DAFx-11 Table 1 & Fig. 4b).
   - Function: Controls the horizontal inflection point of the 2D VPS piecewise phase shaper.

2. vec_v_formant
   - Range: 0.50 to 6.00 (Vertical Inflection Position v, Default: 2.20)
   - Taper: Linear harmonic formant multiplier (at d = 0.5, formant center frequency is f_f = (2*v - 1) * f0, spanning 1x fundamental up to the 11th harmonic).
   - Function: Controls the vertical phase target at the breakpoint, sweeping resonant vocal formants through the tuba's harmonic series.

3. liss_depth
   - Range: 0.0 to 1.0 (2D Lissajous Orbit Radius, Default: 0.35)
   - Taper: Power curve (x^1.25) scaling both Ad (+/-0.38) and Av (+/-1.80).
   - Function: Expands the 2D Lissajous trajectory around (vec_d, vec_v_formant) with 90-degree stereo quadrature between Left and Right channels.

4. liss_ratio
   - Range: 1 to 4 (Integer Vertical-to-Horizontal Lissajous Frequency Ratio w_v : w_d)
   - Taper: Stepped integer ratio (1 = circular/elliptical orbit; 2 = figure-eight; 3 = three vertical rings, DAFx-11 Fig. 7; 4 = four-lobed crown).
   - Function: Sets the harmonic ratio between vertical and horizontal vector modulation oscillators.

5. audio_rate_mod
   - Range: 0.0 to 1.0 (0.0 = 1-Bar Synced LFO Lissajous, 1.0 = Pitch-Locked Audio-Rate Lissajous at f0)
   - Taper: Linear crossfade between control-rate (Sec 4.3) and audio-rate (Sec 5.2, Fig. 12) vector modulation.
   - Function: Raises the Lissajous vector modulation into the audio range locked to the tuba's fundamental f0 for complex curved-phase waveshaping.

6. alias_suppress
   - Range: 0.0 to 1.0 (Default: 0.85)
   - Taper: Linear blend between trivial VPS (0.0) and DAFx-11 Section 3.2 Eq. 11–12 incomplete-period sinusoid folding (1.0).
   - Function: Suppresses phase-wrap discontinuities when 2v - 1 is non-integral during smooth formant sweeps.
   - Only acts for v >= 1 (below that the shaper is one complete period). The paper's two branches (fractional part of v below / above 0.5) produce different shapes at exactly 0.5, so they are crossfaded over 0.35..0.65 to keep the waveform continuous while v sweeps.

7. env_follow
   - Range: 0.0 to 1.0 (0.0 = Gate-Normalized Synth Sustain, 1.0 = Natural Tuba Hilbert Amplitude Envelope)
   - Taper: Linear crossfade.
   - Function: Controls how closely the VPS output follows the tuba's breath dynamics (in6, or the internal follower). The gate side fades in over ~4 ms and out over ~40 ms, so it cannot snap or chatter on a decaying note.

CLICK FIXES (2026-10-25 audit)
- Alias suppression had a jump inside every period when the fractional part of v was above 0.5 (up to 0.69 of full scale at v = 2.6), a jump at the period wrap for v between 0.5 and 1, and switched shape abruptly as v swept through n.5. At the defaults the Lissajous orbit (v 1.72..2.68) hit all three every LFO cycle. All removed: the shaper is continuous for every (d, v).
- Amplitude came from a 2.5/25 ms peak follower that rippled ~12% at f0 on low notes (an AM buzz) and gated with a hard threshold. It now uses in6 or a cascaded follower (<2% ripple), a smoothed gate and a 1 ms output smoother.
- Phase came from a resonant (nonlinear) svf + Hilbert PLL whose uncapped correction could sweep the pitch by ~800 Hz while re-locking on an attack. With in5 the tracker's phase is used directly; the fallback PLL uses a linear bandpass and its correction is capped at +/-25% of the pitch.
- Bar start/stop and liss_ratio steps glide over 5 ms instead of jumping.
- Anti-alias guard: d is kept far enough from 0 and 1 that neither segment of the shaper exceeds 0.2*sr in instantaneous frequency (v/d*f0 and |1-v|/(1-d)*f0). Inactive at normal settings; very small d with a high v still gives a deliberate buzzy burst every cycle.
