================================================================================
chorus_erb_allpass_reson_deco — Parameter Information
================================================================================

OVERVIEW
A standalone stereo Perceptual ERB Allpass & Group-Delay Chorus uniting two DAFx spatial decorrelation papers:
1. Elliot Kermit-Canfield and Jonathan Abel (DAFx-16 Paper 30, Sections 3.1–3.3, Eq. 1–6, Fig. 1–3): Cascades 6 conjugate-symmetric biquad allpass filters with ERB-spaced pole frequencies and mutually prime multi-sample delays (`z^-P`, where Left uses primes `[19, 13, 11, 7, 5, 3]` and Right uses `[23, 17, 7, 11, 3, 5]`) so the phase wraps rapidly around the unit circle while keeping a flat magnitude response (exactly flat at 100% wet with `hf_transient_tightness` at 0). The delays are given for 48 kHz and scale with sample rate and `erb_group_delay_scale`, then snap to the nearest prime (2–61 samples) so the stages stay mutually prime.
2. Jon Fagerström, Nils Meyer-Kahlen, Sebastian J. Schlecht, and Vesa Välimäki (DAFx-25 Paper 80, Sections 3 & 4, Table 1, Eq. 4–14, Fig. 2–4): Borrows the idea of its listening-test-derived ERB profile (Table 1): most group delay in the low/mid bands, much less at high frequencies so brass transients stay tight. This is an approximation, not the paper's resonator design. Measured on this cascade at 48 kHz with default settings: mean group delay ≈ 9 ms at 60–200 Hz, ≈ 3 ms at 200–800 Hz and ≈ 1–1.5 ms above, with small L/R group-delay differences (< 0.4 ms below 2 kHz). The stereo decorrelation comes mainly from the two channels' different phase responses. Because `z^-P` replicates every stage's poles across the whole spectrum, the HF taper is done with a crossover (see `hf_transient_tightness`).

INLETS & OUTLETS
- in1 / in2: Stereo Audio Input (Tuba). If in2 stays silent for 250 ms, in1 is used for both sides.
- in3: Optional Envelope Modulator [0..1] (sweeps ERB pole angles on loud tuba attacks). This is an exception to the repo's in3-pitch convention: values above 20 (a tracked pitch in Hz) are ignored.
- in4: Optional 1-Bar Phasor [0..1] (an exception to the repo's in4-in-Hz convention). While it is moving, drift LFO 1 runs at 1 cycle per bar (LFO 2 stays free at 1.23 × `drift_rate_hz`); 100 ms after it stops, `drift_rate_hz` takes over again.
- out1 / out2: Stereo ERB Perceptual Allpass Chorus Output

PARAMETERS (6)

1. erb_group_delay_scale
   - Range: 0.10 to 1.00 (Prime-Delay Multiplier `z^(-P)` Across the 6 ERB Allpass Stages, Default: 0.70)
   - Taper: Linear scaling of the prime sample delays `P_L` and `P_R`, snapped to the nearest prime (DAFx-16 Section 3.3). Steps rather than sweeps: moving it changes the delays in whole samples and can click, so set it rather than automate it.
   - Function: Controls how many times the phase wraps around the unit circle and so the total group delay (≈ 3 ms at 0.1, ≈ 9 ms at 0.7, ≈ 11 ms at 1.0 for the 60–200 Hz band at default radius). At low settings several stages share the same 2-sample delay.

2. pole_radius_resonance
   - Range: 0.0 to 1.0 (maps to Pole Radius `r` = 0.52 to 0.92, Default: 0.72)
   - Taper: Linear pole radius within the DAFx-16 Eq. 5–6 bounds (`0.5 <= r < beta`).
   - Function: Controls the sharpness and peak group delay `tau(w_k) = 1 / alpha_k` (DAFx-25 Eq. 10, Fig. 4b) of the 6 allpass stages. Low-band mean group delay goes from ≈ 5 ms at 0.0 to ≈ 10 ms at 1.0 (default `erb_group_delay_scale`).

3. pole_drift_depth
   - Range: 0.0 to 1.0 (Time-Varying ERB Pole-Angle Drift Depth, Default: 0.45)
   - Taper: Linear octave sweep depth.
   - Function: At 0.0, operates as a static LTI perceptual stereo widener; above 0.0, gently drifts the 6 ERB pole angles in counter-quadrature between Left and Right for liquid, multi-pole allpass chorusing.

4. drift_rate_hz
   - Range: 0.08 to 3.50 Hz (ERB Pole-Angle Drift Rate, Default: 0.42 Hz)
   - Taper: Direct frequency in Hz.
   - Function: Sets the speed of the counter-rotating ERB pole modulation.

5. hf_transient_tightness
   - Range: 0.0 to 1.0 (DAFx-25 Table 1 High-Frequency Group-Delay Taper, Default: 0.75)
   - Taper: Moves a complementary 2-pole crossover from 12 kHz (0.0) down to 1.5 kHz (1.0) and routes that fraction of the band above it around the allpass cascade; it also slightly lowers the pole radii of the upper three stages. Default 0.75 ≈ 2.5 kHz crossover.
   - Function: Follows the DAFx-25 finding that less group delay in the upper bands keeps high-frequency transients crisp while the low/mid range stays wide. At 0.0 the whole signal is allpassed (fully flat magnitude); higher settings keep brass edges undelayed, with some coloration around the crossover where the delayed and undelayed parts overlap.

6. dry_wet_chorus_mix
   - Range: 0.0 to 1.0 (Default: 0.50 = Dense Multi-Notch Allpass Chorus; 1.00 = Pure Flat-Magnitude Perceptual Stereo Widener!)
   - Taper: Constant-power cosine/sine crossfade.
   - Function: Because the 6 prime-delay biquad stages are strictly allpass, setting `dry_wet_chorus_mix = 1.00` (with `hf_transient_tightness` at 0) gives a flat-magnitude stereo widener with no comb coloration, while `0.50` sums dry and allpassed signals into a dense phase-interference chorus (each channel's cascade has 2 × ΣP poles, 82 at default settings).
