================================================================================
chorus_bbd_blep_clock_matrix — Parameter Information
================================================================================

OVERVIEW
A standalone stereo BBD Chorus/Flanger Matrix combining Leonardo Gabrielli, Stefano D'Angelo, and Stefano Squartini's "Antialiasing in BBD Chips Using BLEP" (DAFx-25 Paper 29, Sections 2.1, 3 & 4.3, Algorithm 1, Eq. 1–2) with Jaromír Mačák's Electro-Harmonix Deluxe Electric Mistress nonlinear RC astable clock generator (DAFx-16 Paper 59, Sections 2.1–2.2, Eq. 8–11). Instead of approximating a BBD with a smooth digital delay line, this effect clocks a true 256-bucket discrete-time shift register at F_CLK(t). When F_CLK is lowered into the audio range (4–15 kHz), it produces authentic BBD-Generated Aliasing (BGA: musical foldback sidebands at k*F_CLK +/- f0), while a continuous-time PolyBLEP step residual eliminates harsh digital Simulation-Generated Aliasing (SGA) at every sample-and-hold transition!

INLETS & OUTLETS
- in1 / in2: Stereo Audio Input (Tuba). If in2 stays silent for 250 ms, in1 is used for both sides.
- in3: Optional Envelope Modulator [0..1] (shifts the astable comparator threshold U_THD on loud attacks)
- in4: Optional 1-Bar Phasor [0..1] (an exception to the repo's in4-in-Hz convention). While it is moving, the sweep runs at exactly 1 cycle per bar and `lfo_rate_hz` is ignored; 100 ms after it stops moving, `lfo_rate_hz` takes over again.
- out1 / out2: Stereo PolyBLEP Clock-Stepped BBD Chorus Output

PARAMETERS (7)

1. lfo_rate_hz
   - Range: 0.08 to 5.00 Hz (LFO Sweep Rate, Default: 0.45 Hz)
   - Taper: Direct frequency in Hz.
   - Function: Controls the speed of the triangle integrator/comparator LFO (DAFx-16 Eq. 4).

2. sweep_depth
   - Range: 0.0 to 1.0 (Clock Excursion Depth, Default: 0.55)
   - Taper: Linear modulation depth.
   - Function: Controls how wide the BBD clock frequency swings around bbd_clock_khz. Also scales the in3 envelope push. At 0.0 the clock (and so the delay) is static, which with `matrix_feedback` gives the Electric Mistress "Filter Matrix" style fixed metallic comb (DAFx-16 Section 3).

3. bbd_clock_khz
   - Range: 4.0 to 48.0 kHz (Nominal BBD Clock Frequency F_CLK, Default: 18.0 kHz -> ~14.2 ms chorus delay across 256 buckets)
   - Taper: Direct clock frequency in kHz.
   - Function: High clocks (25–48 kHz, 5–10 ms delay) yield pristine, silvery stereo chorusing/flanging; lower clocks (4–12 kHz, 21–64 ms delay) intentionally expose gritty, musical BBD clock-foldback aliasing (BGA) while PolyBLEP keeps the digital simulation clean!

4. astable_curve
   - Range: 0.0 to 1.0 (Nonlinear RC Astable Charging Law, Default: 0.55)
   - Taper: Linear blend between symmetric sweep (0.0) and Mačák's logarithmic capacitor charging law T_clock = -2*tau*ln((U_Max - U_THD)/U_Max) (1.0, DAFx-16 Eq. 9).
   - Function: Imparts the unmistakable "lingering at the top, swooping at the bottom" sweep trajectory of vintage EHX BBD modulation pedals.

5. matrix_feedback
   - Range: -0.85 to +0.85 (BBD Regeneration / Color Feedback, Default: +0.30)
   - Taper: Linear bipolar feedback with internal tanh saturation guard.
   - Function: Emphasizes the BBD comb resonances for hollow Deluxe Electric Mistress / metallic dubstep chorus textures.

6. recon_filter_hz
   - Range: 1500.0 to 18000.0 Hz (Post-BBD Anti-Imaging Reconstruction Lowpass Cutoff, Default: 7500.0 Hz)
   - Taper: Direct frequency in Hz.
   - Function: Controls how much of the BBD clock-foldback sparkle (BGA) passes into the stereo output.

7. dry_wet_mix
   - Range: 0.0 to 1.0 (0% Dry to 100% Wet; Default: 0.50 = 50/50 Chorus)
   - Taper: Constant-power cosine/sine crossfade.
   - Function: Balances the dry tuba against the PolyBLEP BBD chorus.

IMPLEMENTATION NOTES
- The BBD input is always driven through `tanh(input + feedback)`, so hot input saturates the bucket chain even with feedback at 0, like a real BBD input stage.
- The PolyBLEP correction is the two-sided (2-sample) residual, applied with a one-sample output delay so the sample before each clock step is corrected too. The wet path therefore has one extra sample of latency (about 0.02 ms).
