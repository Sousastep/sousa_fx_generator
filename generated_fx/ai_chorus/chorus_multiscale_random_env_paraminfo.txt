================================================================================
chorus_multiscale_random_env — Parameter Information
================================================================================

OVERVIEW
A standalone stereo Multiscale Chorus implementing all three digital chorus enhancements from P. Fernández-Cid and F. J. Casajús-Quirós (DAFx-98 Paper FER09, Sections 5, 6, 7 & 8):
1. Raised-Cosine Interpolated Narrowband Random Modulation Law (Section 5): Generates a random process at sub-audio rate f_sm, interpolates smoothly between random targets with a raised-cosine (Hann) curve (continuous value and slope), and multiplies point-by-point with a cosine carrier to blend continuously from periodic sinusoidal chorusing to organic, non-repeating ensemble drift.
2. Multiscale Dyadic Filterbank Chorus (Section 7): Splits the tuba into 3 octave scales (Low < 250 Hz, Mid 250–1250 Hz, High > 1250 Hz) with independent modulated delay lines per band—keeping the low tuba fundamental tight while richly chorusing the midrange and bell overtones.
3. Per-Band Envelope Automation (Sections 6 & 8): Each scale's envelope follower automates its own wet/dry mix (leaving quiet bands unprocessed so background breath/valve noise is never timbrally modulated) and dynamically increases randomness and tonal center on loud blasts.

INLETS & OUTLETS
- in1 / in2: Stereo Audio Input (Tuba). If in2 stays silent for 250 ms, in1 is used for both sides.
- in3: Unused / Thru
- in4: Optional 1-Bar Phasor [0..1] (an exception to the repo's in4-in-Hz convention). While it is moving, the low-scale carrier runs at 1 cycle per bar (the mid and high carriers stay free at 1.0 × and 1.33 × `central_rate_hz`); 100 ms after it stops, the free carrier takes over.
- out1 / out2: Stereo Multiscale Chorus Output

PARAMETERS (7)

1. central_rate_hz
   - Range: 0.15 to 5.00 Hz (Central Modulation Frequency, Default: 0.85 Hz)
   - Taper: Direct frequency in Hz (DAFx-98 Section 5).
   - Function: Sets the perceived central speed of the modulation law whether sinusoidal or random.

2. randomness_bw
   - Range: 0.0 to 1.0 (0.0 = Pure Sinusoidal LFO, 1.0 = Full Raised-Cosine Narrowband Random LFO, Default: 0.55)
   - Taper: Linear blend + bandwidth expansion of the sub-audio random sampling rate f_sm (DAFx-98 Section 5).
   - Function: Eliminates the repetitive "seasick LFO cycle" of standard choruses while staying click-free: the raised-cosine interpolator has no jumps in value or slope (its curvature does change at each new random target, which is inaudible at these sub-audio rates). Higher settings also raise the random target rate, and loud playing raises it further (Section 8).

3. delay_depth_ms
   - Range: 2.0 to 25.0 ms (Nominal Chorus Delay & Excursion Scale, Default: 8.5 ms)
   - Taper: Direct delay time in milliseconds.
   - Function: Scales both the mean delay and modulation excursion across all three octave scales (from 2 ms flanger-chorus to 25 ms wide ensemble doubling).

4. scale_independence
   - Range: 0.0 to 1.0 (0.0 = All 3 Scales Share One Modulation Law, 1.0 = Fully Independent Modulation Laws per Scale, Default: 0.75)
   - Taper: Linear crossfade (DAFx-98 Section 7).
   - Function: As shown on page 5 of DAFx-98, sharing a single modulation law across all scales (0.0) imparts a "metallic/tonal" comb character, whereas independent modulation laws per scale (1.0) produce a smooth, non-metallic phaser/ensemble texture!

5. env_mix_duck_noise
   - Range: 0.0 to 1.0 (Per-Band Envelope-Driven Wet Mix Gating, Default: 0.45)
   - Taper: Linear sensitivity (DAFx-98 Sections 6 & 7).
   - Function: Frequency bands with little energy remain dry so steady background/preamp noise is never modulated into "swishing" chorus noise during rests.

6. env_color_shift
   - Range: 0.0 to 1.0 (Envelope-Driven Main Delay / Tonal Center Shift, Default: 0.35)
   - Taper: Linear modulation depth (DAFx-98 Section 8).
   - Function: Shortens the fixed main delay portion on loud notes, shifting the tonal center of each band in proportion to playing intensity.

7. dry_wet_mix
   - Range: 0.0 to 1.0 (Chorus Wet Level, Default: 0.55)
   - Taper: Linear wet gain summed on top of the full dry signal (the dry bands always pass at unity, so 1.0 is dry + full wet, not wet only).
   - Function: Sets the overall intensity of the multiscale chorus voices.
