================================================================================
chorus_bbd_compander_juno — Parameter Information
================================================================================

OVERVIEW
A standalone stereo analog Bucket-Brigade Device (BBD) Chorus uniting three DAFx virtual-analog papers:
1. Antti Huovilainen (DAFx-05 Paper P_155, Sections 5.1 & 5.2, Eq. 25–31, Fig. 8–11): Models the fixed-bucket-count variable-clock delay law (where a linear triangle LFO warps into an asymmetric 1/f_clock hyperbola smoothed by the bucket transit time, Eq. 25) and a matched NE570-style feedback compressor / feedforward expander pair (implemented as a gentler 1.5:1 / 1:1.5 square-root law).
2. Colin Raffel and Julius O. Smith (DAFx-10 Paper P42, Sections 2.2, 3.3 & 4.4): Models clock-tracking Sallen-Key anti-aliasing and reconstruction lowpass filters plus stage-accumulated MOSFET charge-transfer harmonic warmth (approximated by an asymmetric tanh).
3. Martin Holters and Julian D. Parker (DAFx-18 Paper 12, Sections 2 & 4): Models the classic Roland Juno-60 / Dimension-D push-pull stereo BBD chorus architecture (single-LFO Mode I push-pull and a dual-LFO I+II ensemble).

INLETS & OUTLETS
- in1 / in2: Stereo Audio Input (Tuba). If in2 stays silent for 250 ms, in1 is used for both sides.
- in3: Unused / Thru (standalone chorus requires no external pitch tracker)
- in4: Optional 1-Bar Phasor [0..1] (an exception to the repo's in4-in-Hz convention). While it is moving, the primary triangle LFO runs at 1 cycle per bar and `lfo_rate_hz` only sets the secondary LFO (1.618 × rate + 0.45 Hz); 100 ms after it stops, the free LFO takes over.
- out1 / out2: Stereo Analog BBD Chorus Output

PARAMETERS (7)

1. lfo_rate_hz
   - Range: 0.10 to 5.00 Hz (Primary BBD Triangle LFO Rate, Default: 0.55 Hz, matching Roland Juno-60 Mode I = 0.51 Hz / Mode II = 0.86 Hz)
   - Taper: Direct frequency in Hz.
   - Function: Sets the sweep speed of the BBD clock generator.

2. clock_mod_depth
   - Range: 0.0 to 1.0 (BBD Clock Frequency Modulation Depth, Default: 0.60)
   - Taper: Linear clock modulation index (warped via 1 / (1 + m*LFO) per Huovilainen DAFx-05 Fig. 9).
   - Function: Controls the excursion of the variable BBD clock, producing warm, asymmetrical pitch thickening.

3. center_delay_ms
   - Range: 1.50 to 15.00 ms (Nominal MN3007 512-Stage BBD Delay Time, Default: 3.60 ms, matching the Juno-60's 3.45 ms center delay)
   - Taper: Direct delay time in milliseconds (determines nominal clock frequency f_clock = 512 / (2 * delay)).
   - Function: Short delays (1.5–4.0 ms) deliver tight Roland Juno/Dimension-D stereo widening; longer delays (6–15 ms) produce lush 1970s Boss CE-1 ensemble doubling.

4. juno_mode_morph
   - Range: 0.0 to 1.0 (Default: 0.35)
   - Taper: Continuous morph between:
     * 0.0: Pure Push-Pull Anti-Phase Triangle LFO (Juno Mode I / Dimension-D spatial widening with zero mono pitch wobble)
     * 1.0: Dual-Rate Incommensurate LFO Sum (65 % primary + 35 % secondary at 1.618 × rate + 0.45 Hz; Juno I+II style ensemble swirl)
     * In between: a linear blend of the two (there is no separate Mode II speed setting; use `lfo_rate_hz` ≈ 0.86 Hz for Mode II)
   - Function: Morphs the modulation law from single-LFO push-pull stereo to dual-LFO ensemble movement.

5. ne570_compand
   - Range: 0.0 to 1.0 (NE570 Compander Depth, Default: 0.55)
   - Taper: Linear wet/dry blend of the 1.5:1 feedback compressor before the BBD and 1:1.5 feedforward expander after the BBD (a square-root version of DAFx-05 Eq. 26–27's 2:1 law). At 1.0 the pair is level-matched for normal playing levels; very quiet tails are expanded slightly below their input level.
   - Function: Reproduces the dynamic "breathing" and transient punch of analog BBD pedals equipped with Philips NE570/SA571 compander ICs.

6. bbd_warmth_thd
   - Range: 0.0 to 1.0 (BBD Sallen-Key Roll-Off & MOSFET Charge-Transfer THD, Default: 0.45)
   - Taper: Linear warmth control.
   - Function: Simultaneously darkens the clock-tracking anti-aliasing/reconstruction lowpass filters and increases asymmetrical bucket-stage harmonic saturation (DAFx-10 Sec 4.4).

7. dry_wet_mix
   - Range: 0.0 to 1.0 (0% Dry to 100% Wet Vibrato; Default: 0.50 = Classic 50/50 Stereo Chorus)
   - Taper: Constant-power cosine/sine crossfade.
   - Function: At 0.50, creates full stereo BBD chorusing; at 1.00, isolates the wet BBD path for true pitch vibrato.
