================================================================================
chorus_velvet_noise_ensemble — Parameter Information
================================================================================

OVERVIEW
A standalone stereo Velvet-Noise Ensemble Chorus & Spatial Decorrelator implementing Benoit Alary, Archontis Politis, and Vesa Välimäki's "Velvet-Noise Decorrelator" (DAFx-17 Paper 96, Sections 2.1, 2.2 & 3.1–3.3, Eq. 1–12, Fig. 1–4). Standard multi-tap choruses suffer from regular comb-filter coloration and transient smearing. This effect convolves Left and Right channels with two mutually orthogonal 16-impulse `{-1, 0, +1}` Velvet-Noise Sequences (VNS) across a 5–35 ms window using:
1. Logarithmic Impulse Distribution (Section 3.3, Eq. 11–12, Fig. 4): Concentrates the velvet impulses near the start of the sequence so loud early impulses widen the tuba immediately without smearing tongue transients.
2. 4-Segment Staircase Decay Envelope (Section 3.2, Eq. 10, Fig. 3): Applies 4 progressive attenuation weights `s0..s3` to prevent late-tap echo flutter while normalizing total RMS power.
3. Quadrature Tap Micro-Modulation (`tap_shimmer_depth`): Gently modulates the 16 sparse velvet tap positions with multi-phase quadrature LFOs, transforming the static velvet-noise decorrelator into a dense, comb-free 16-voice stereo ensemble!

INLETS & OUTLETS
- in1 / in2: Stereo Audio Input (Tuba). If in2 stays silent for 250 ms, in1 is used for both sides.
- in3: Optional Transient Envelope [0..1] (shortens `sequence_length_ms` by up to 35 % on hard attacks to keep staccato articulation sharp). The taps move with it, so a fast envelope gives a brief pitch blip; feed it a smoothed envelope. This is an exception to the repo's in3-pitch convention: values above 20 (a tracked pitch in Hz) are ignored.
- in4: Optional 1-Bar Phasor [0..1] (an exception to the repo's in4-in-Hz convention). While it is moving, shimmer LFO 1 (left taps) runs at 1 cycle per bar; LFO 2 (right taps) stays free at 1.31 × `shimmer_rate_hz`. 100 ms after it stops, `shimmer_rate_hz` takes over again.
- out1 / out2: Stereo Velvet-Noise Ensemble Chorus Output

PARAMETERS (7)

1. sequence_length_ms
   - Range: 5.0 to 35.0 ms (Total Velvet-Noise Filter Span `Ls`, Default: 22.0 ms, inside the 20–30 ms range DAFx-17 Section 1 cites for noise decorrelators; the paper's own examples use 30 ms)
   - Taper: Direct sequence length in milliseconds.
   - Function: Controls the spatial extent of the velvet-noise ensemble without crossing into discrete reverberation (>35 ms). Below ~10 ms the 16 taps sit inside one period of the tuba's fundamental, so the wet path loses some deep bass (up to ~10 dB at 25–60 Hz on the right channel at 5 ms); 20 ms and above stay within a few dB of flat.

2. log_tap_concentration
   - Range: 0.0 to 1.0 (0.0 = Uniform Grid Spacing Eq. 4, 1.0 = Logarithmic Grid Spacing Eq. 11–12; Default: 0.65)
   - Taper: Linear blend between uniform and logarithmic impulse spacing (DAFx-17 Fig. 3 vs. Fig. 4).
   - Function: Packs a higher density of impulses into the first 2–8 ms of the sequence to preserve crisp brass transients.

3. segment_decay_db
   - Range: 3.0 to 24.0 dB (Total Attenuation `L_dB` Across the 4 Staircase Segments, Default: 12.0 dB)
   - Taper: Logarithmic dB decay across segments `s0, s1, s2, s3` with automatic RMS power normalization (DAFx-17 Section 3.1 & 3.2, Eq. 7 & 10).
   - Function: Controls how quickly later velvet taps decay relative to early taps.

4. tap_shimmer_depth
   - Range: 0.0 to 1.0 (maps to 0.0 to +/- 1.25 ms Per-Tap Quadrature Delay Modulation, Default: 0.45)
   - Taper: Linear modulation excursion.
   - Function: At 0.0, operates as a pure time-invariant DAFx-17 Velvet-Noise Decorrelator (zero pitch change, huge headphone/speaker externalization); above 0.0, animates all 16 taps into a lush, swirling 16-voice ensemble chorus.

5. shimmer_rate_hz
   - Range: 0.10 to 4.00 Hz (Velvet Tap Shimmer LFO Rate, Default: 0.75 Hz)
   - Taper: Direct frequency in Hz.
   - Function: Sets the modulation speed of the 16 velvet taps.

6. stereo_decorrelation
   - Range: 0.0 to 1.0 (Left/Right Velvet Impulse Position & Polarity Difference, Default: 1.00)
   - Taper: Linear crossfade of both the impulse positions and the impulse signs between identical Left/Right sequences (0.0, mono center) and two different low-coherence sequences (1.0, minimum IACC). Around 0.5 the taps whose signs differ fade out, so the wet level dips by up to ~3 dB there.
   - Function: Controls the apparent sound source width (ASW) between Left and Right speakers.

7. dry_wet_mix
   - Range: 0.0 to 1.0 (0% Dry to 100% Wet Velvet Ensemble; Default: 0.55)
   - Taper: Constant-power cosine/sine crossfade.
   - Function: Balances the center dry tuba with the 16-tap velvet-noise stereo field.
