================================================================================
triode_bias_shift — Parameter Information
================================================================================

WHAT IT IS
A cascade of 12AX7 preamp stages, with the misbehaviour left in. When a hard
note drives a grid positive, grid current charges the coupling cap and
shoves the next notes toward cutoff: the stage chokes, then breathes back
over blocking_ms. On tuba that is the farty, compressed bloom of an amp
pushed past its sweet spot. Asymmetric triode curves give a strong 2nd
harmonic; more stages = more gain and fizz.

Source: Macak & Schimmel, DAFx-10 P12 §3–5, §10 (triode and grid-current
models, coupling-cap state); Dunkel et al., DAFx-16 paper 53 §2.2, §3.4
(Bassman cascaded stages, triode model); Cohen & Hélie, DAFx-10 P45 §3.3
(grid current).

PARAMETERS (5 + output gain)

1. drive_db — 0…40 dB, default 20 dB
   Level into the first stage. Even 0 dB breaks up with 2+ stages.

2. bias — 0…1, default 0.5
   Operating point. 0 = cold (near cutoff: sputtery, gated, buzzy);
   1 = hot (near grid conduction: fatter, earlier blocking).

3. blocking_ms — 5…500 ms, default 60 ms
   Coupling-cap recovery time. Measured after a loud 110 Hz burst: 5 ms =
   no choke; 60 ms = about 15 dB dip for ~100 ms; 500 ms = silence for
   ~300 ms, then a slow swell back.

4. stages — 1…3 (stepped), default 2
   Number of triode stages in series (later stages get ×3 gain).

5. mix — 0…1, default 1
   Dry/wet (polarity is corrected for odd stage counts).

6. output_db — -36…+12 dB, default 0 dB
   Final trim, last in the chain. Does not count toward the 8-parameter limit.

INLETS / OUTLETS
- in1 / in2: stereo tuba.  out1 / out2: stereo.
- in3 / in4 are not used.

NOTES
- The cutoff ceiling is scaled to -6 dBFS at any bias; about -18 dB RMS out
  with the tuba recording at the defaults.
- A 10 kHz lowpass per stage stands in for Miller capacitance; a 10 Hz
  output coupling cap removes the DC that blocking produces.
- The triode curve is not antialiased (it has no closed-form antiderivative);
  its knees are soft, so aliasing stays low at moderate drive.
- No safety limiter.
