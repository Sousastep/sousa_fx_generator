================================================================================
simper_overdrive — Parameter Information
================================================================================

WHAT IT IS
Op-amp overdrive with two antiparallel diodes in the feedback loop (the
Tube Screamer topology). The diode network is solved exactly every sample
instead of being approximated by tanh, so the knee is the real
exponential diode curve. The clean horn always passes and the diodes add
at most a diode drop (~0.7 V) on top of it. Push the drive hard and the
op amp runs into its 9 V rails as well.

Port of a gen~ codebox by quails, from a lecture by Andrew Simper (Cytomic),
with solver hardening by sousastep. The original's 4x oversampling is not
ported: this runs at 1x, so expect some aliasing at high gain and use
tone_hz to tame it.

Source: Yeh, Abel & Smith, DAFx-07 p189 §4.2 (non-inverting op amp with diode
limiter), eq. 17 / Fig. 10 (diode model); Albertini, Bernardini & Sarti,
JAES 2021 §7.2 (Tube Screamer clipping stage, asymmetric diodes).

GAIN STAGING
The effect is level-matched. Whenever drive_db, gain_db, tight_hz or asym
change, it solves the circuit for a -12 dBFS, 200 Hz reference and sets a
makeup gain so that reference comes out at -12 dBFS. Drive and gain change
how much it clips, not how loud it is, so mix blends at matched level.
Quieter playing comes up and louder playing is held back, which is the
compression you hear. Use output_db for the final level.

PARAMETERS (7)

1. drive_db — -24…36 dB, default 0 dB
   Input level into the circuit (0 dB = 1 V per digital full scale, as in
   the original). Around 0 dB: diode overdrive. Above ~+12 dB: the op amp
   also hits its rails, which sounds harder and fuzzier. Below 0 dB: cleaner,
   only the peaks break up.

2. gain_db — 0…40 dB, default 20 dB
   Op-amp gain, 20·log10(1 + R2/R1), the pedal's Drive knob and the
   original's `in2` gain (gain 9 = 20 dB). 0 dB = clean.

3. tight_hz — 10…1000 Hz, default 10 Hz
   Corner of the capacitor in the op amp's ground leg. Only content above it
   reaches the diodes; below it the horn passes clean. 10 Hz = the original
   (full-range clipping, fat). A real Tube Screamer sits at ~720 Hz: the
   fundamental stays round and only the bark clips.

4. asym — 0…100 %, default 0 %
   Adds series diodes on the positive side (100 % = two diodes against one).
   The positive half then clips later than the negative half, which adds
   even harmonics for a warmer, more tube-like sound. 0 % = the original
   (symmetric). A 10 Hz coupling cap removes the DC this creates.

5. tone_hz — 500…20000 Hz, default 20000 Hz
   Lowpass after the clipper. 20 kHz ≈ off (the original). Turn it down to
   darken the sound and to hide aliasing at high gain.

6. mix — 0…100 %, default 100 %
   Dry/wet, level-matched at the reference (see GAIN STAGING). Parallel
   overdrive (30–60 %) keeps the tuba's attack and low end.

7. output_db — -24…24 dB, default 0 dB
   Final trim after the level matching.

PORT NOTES
- Same circuit and fixed point as the gen~ original (Is = 1e-15 A,
  Vt = 26 mV, R1 = 10k, R2 = gain·10k), solved for the diode voltage with a
  warm-started, bracketed Newton solve. It stops early once a step is below
  1e-12 V (20 iterations max, 60 for the cold level-matching solve).
- Stereo: each channel keeps its own warm-start seed.
- Added to the circuit: the ground-leg capacitor (tight_hz), asymmetric
  diodes (asym), soft ±4.5 V op-amp rails, and the tone lowpass.
- The gen~ dcblock is replaced by a 10 Hz one-pole high-pass (output coupling
  cap).
- Level matching is static: it assumes the reference sits in the gain leg's
  passband scaled by the tight_hz response at 200 Hz. With high tight_hz
  settings, very low notes come out a little quieter than the reference.
- in3 (pitch) and in4 (bar phasor) are not used.
