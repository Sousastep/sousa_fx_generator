================================================================================
rectifier_sag — Parameter Information
================================================================================

WHAT IT IS
Power-supply sag from a tube rectifier. The harder you play, the more
current the power stage pulls and the further the B+ rail droops: the
attack of a note hits at full voltage, then the sustain compresses, clips
harder and plays quieter, and the amp blooms back once you let go. Both
channels share one supply, as in a real amp. Antialiased clipping.

Source: Macak & Schimmel, DAFx-11 (2011_05_e) §3 eq. 13–14 (supply node:
reservoir cap charged through the supply resistance, drained by the tube
currents); Macak & Schimmel, DAFx-10 P12 §2 (power supply in the amp's block
diagram).

PARAMETERS (4 + output gain)

1. drive_db — 0…40 dB, default 18 dB
   Level into the power stage. Sag only bites once the stage is working
   hard, so it scales with this.

2. sag_pct — 0…100 %, default 60 %
   Rectifier/supply resistance. 0 = stiff solid-state supply (no sag).
   Measured on a sustained loud 110 Hz note: about 4.4 dB of droop at 60 %,
   6.6 dB at 100 %, with harder clipping as it sags.

3. recovery_ms — 20…1000 ms, default 150 ms
   Reservoir-cap time constant: how fast the rail droops and recovers.
   Short = tight, pumping chug; long = slow, spongy swell.

4. mix — 0…1, default 1
   Dry/wet.

5. output_db — -36…+12 dB, default 0 dB
   Final trim, last in the chain. Does not count toward the 8-parameter limit.

INLETS / OUTLETS
- in1 / in2: stereo tuba.  out1 / out2: stereo.
- in3 / in4 are not used.

NOTES
- Headroom follows the rail and gain follows its square root, so a sagging
  rail clips harder rather than just turning down.
- About -22 dB RMS out with the -32 dB RMS tuba recording at the defaults.
- 10 Hz DC block after the stage.
- No safety limiter.
