================================================================================
pushpull_crossover — Parameter Information
================================================================================

WHAT IT IS
A push-pull pentode power stage. Each tube handles one half of the wave; with
little idle current neither is really on as the wave crosses zero, so every
crossing gets a small notch: crossover distortion. It is worst on quiet
signals, so loud notes sound like a cranked amp while decays and soft notes
turn spitty and gated. Mismatched tubes add even harmonics; global negative
feedback smooths the notch and tightens the clipping.

Source: Macak & Schimmel, DAFx-11 (2011_05_e) §2.1, §3 (pentode model,
push-pull circuit); Cohen & Hélie, DAFx-10 P45 §3.2 (Koren tube model).

PARAMETERS (5 + output gain)

1. drive_db — 0…40 dB, default 18 dB
   Level into the power tubes. Low = clean-ish with crossover grit; high =
   full plate clipping.

2. bias_pct — 0…100 %, default 15 %
   Idle current. 0 = past class B (a small dead band: maximum spit); ~17 =
   class B; 100 = warm class AB (no notch). Measured on a quiet 55 Hz sine:
   THD -21 dB at the default, -47 dB at 100 %.

3. mismatch_pct — 0…50 %, default 10 %
   One tube weaker and biased differently from the other: adds 2nd harmonic
   and an uneven, lopsided crossover.

4. negfb_pct — 0…100 %, default 30 %
   Global negative feedback around the stage. More = less notch, tighter and
   a little quieter; 0 = loose and raw.

5. mix — 0…1, default 1
   Dry/wet.

6. output_db — -36…+12 dB, default 0 dB
   Final trim, last in the chain. Does not count toward the 8-parameter limit.

INLETS / OUTLETS
- in1 / in2: stereo tuba.  out1 / out2: stereo.
- in3 / in4 are not used.

NOTES
- The plate limit is -6 dBFS; about -26 dB RMS out with the -32 dB RMS tuba
  recording at the defaults (drive_db 30 brings it near -15 dB RMS).
- No output transformer here: chain transformer_flux_sat after it for that.
- 10 Hz DC block after the pair. Not antialiased (soft curves).
- No safety limiter.
