================================================================================
ge_fuzz_starved — Parameter Information
================================================================================

WHAT IT IS
A germanium Fuzz Face. Two transistor stages clip asymmetrically (strong
2nd harmonic, woolly and round), and the input cap charges through the
transistor's base on hard notes, so the fuzz chokes and sputters as notes
decay. Turn the battery down for the "dying battery" sound: the bias slides
toward cutoff, so only the peaks get through, gated and spitting. Leakage
pushes the other way, toward a mushy, compressed fuzz. Antialiased.

Source: Holmes, Holters & van Walstijn, DAFx-17 paper 28 §2.1, §4
(Ebers–Moll germanium BJT, Fuzz-Face case study); Holmes & van Walstijn,
DAFx-16 paper 28 §3.2 (bias parameters dominate the sound).

PARAMETERS (5 + output gain)

1. fuzz_pct — 0…100 %, default 70 %
   Gain of the second transistor (the Fuzz knob). 0 = a dirty boost, much
   quieter; 100 = full square-wave fuzz.

2. battery_v — 3…9.6 V, default 9 V
   Supply voltage. Below ~7 V the bias starves: gated, sputtery, thinner and
   quieter (at 3 V about 20 dB quieter than at 9 V; only the loudest notes
   pass).

3. leakage_pct — 0…100 %, default 20 %
   Germanium leakage current (a warm transistor). More = bias pushed toward
   saturation, darker and more compressed.

4. input_trim_db — -24…+12 dB, default 0 dB
   Level into the pedal. Like rolling back a guitar's volume knob, turning it
   down cleans the fuzz up.

5. mix — 0…1, default 1
   Dry/wet.

6. output_db — -36…+12 dB, default 0 dB
   Final trim, last in the chain. Does not count toward the 8-parameter limit.

INLETS / OUTLETS
- in1 / in2: stereo tuba.  out1 / out2: stereo.
- in3 / in4 are not used.

NOTES
- Silence in = silence out at any bias (each stage is referenced to its
  idle point, so changing the battery/leakage at rest makes no thump).
- Input cap: 2 ms charge, 150 ms release. Output cap: 30 Hz.
- About -14 dB RMS out with the -32 dB RMS tuba recording at the defaults.
- No safety limiter.
