================================================================================
aged_supply_ripple — Parameter Information
================================================================================

WHAT IT IS
An old amp whose filter caps have dried out. The supply can't hold its
voltage between rectifier pulses any more, so a 100/120 Hz sawtooth ripple
rides the rail, and the harder you play the deeper it gets: hum and
gritty sidebands bloom inside your notes and vanish when you stop (silence
in = silence out, no idle hum). A worn decoupling cap lets the rail feed
back into the preamp: an attack bounces, and turned up far enough the amp
motorboats, putt-putting at about 8 Hz while you hold a note.

Source: Macak & Schimmel, DAFx-11 (2011_05_e) §3 eq. 13–14 (supply node as
a state charged through R, drained by the tubes); Dempwolf, Holters, Möller &
Zölzer, DAFx-09 paper 29 §4.3 (component drift changes the sound). The ripple
and motorboat mechanisms extend that supply model to aged parts; no paper in
the corpus models them directly.

PARAMETERS (5 + output gain)

1. drive_db — 0…40 dB, default 15 dB
   Level into the amp. More drive = more current = more ripple and sag.

2. mains_hz — 45…65 Hz, default 60 Hz
   Mains frequency; the ripple runs at twice this (50 = European 100 Hz hum,
   60 = US 120 Hz).

3. cap_age_pct — 0…100 %, default 50 %
   How dried-out the reservoir cap is: ripple depth and sag. Measured on a
   sustained 58 Hz note, 120 Hz hum vs the note: -44 dB at 0, -24 dB at 50,
   -18 dB at 100 (sidebands at 62/178 Hz: -52 / -29 / -21 dB).

4. motorboat_pct — 0…100 %, default 20 %
   Decoupling feedback. Below ~55 % an attack bounces (2–4 dB, settling in
   ~150 ms); from ~60 % the amp self-oscillates at ~8 Hz while you play
   (up to about ±5 dB swing at 100 %).

5. mix — 0…1, default 1
   Dry/wet.

6. output_db — -36…+12 dB, default 0 dB
   Final trim, last in the chain. Does not count toward the 8-parameter limit.

INLETS / OUTLETS
- in1 / in2: stereo tuba.  out1 / out2: stereo.
- in3 / in4 are not used.

NOTES
- The ripple is a fixed mains hum, not pitch-locked: it is meant to clash.
- One supply feeds both channels. 10 Hz DC block; antialiased clipping.
- About -24 dB RMS out with the -32 dB RMS tuba recording at the defaults.
- No safety limiter.
