================================================================================
bitcrush_pitch_hold — Parameter Information
================================================================================

WHAT IT IS
A bitcrusher whose sample-rate reduction follows your pitch. The hold clock
runs at an exact multiple of the tuba's f0, so all the aliasing lands on
harmonics of the note: a crunchy, chiptune-ish buzz that stays in tune
instead of the usual clangorous fog. Bit-depth reduction adds the grainy
staircase (and gates the quietest tails). It can also crush harder toward
the middle of every bar.

Source: Werner, DAFx-13 "Bit Bending" §2.2, §3.4.3–3.4.4 (bit-depth and
clock bends); Parker, Zavalishin & Le Bivic, DAFx-16 paper 41 §1–2
(continuous-time treatment of discontinuities, used here as polyBLEP steps).
The pitch-locked clock is this effect's own design.

PARAMETERS (5 + output gain)

1. bits — 1…16, default 6
   Word length (fractional values allowed). Quantization is relative to
   full scale, so quiet playing crushes harder; 16 = practically clean.

2. hold_hz — 200…48000 Hz, default 4000 Hz
   Hold clock when in3 = 0 (no tracker): a classic, unlocked downsampler.
   48000 (at 48 kHz) = no hold.

3. pitch_ratio — 1…64, default 16
   Hold clock = f0 × this, when in3 > 0. Low = fat, square-ish chip bass
   (4 = four steps per cycle); high = finer grain. Measured on a 55 Hz sine:
   off-harmonic energy -46 dB locked vs -26 dB for a fixed 4 kHz clock.

4. bar_sweep — 0…1, default 0
   Drops up to 8 bits over each bar on a smooth hump (deepest mid-bar, no
   jump at the wrap). Needs a moving in4; with in4 held it does nothing.

5. mix — 0…1, default 1
   Dry/wet (the dry is delayed one sample to stay in phase).

6. output_db — -36…+12 dB, default 0 dB
   Final trim, last in the chain. Does not count toward the 8-parameter limit.

INLETS / OUTLETS
- in1 / in2: stereo tuba.  out1 / out2: stereo.
- in3: f0 in Hz. > 0 locks the clock to pitch_ratio × f0 (f0 clamped
  20…2000 Hz); 0 = disconnected -> hold_hz. No zero-crossing fallback.
- in4: 0..1 one-bar phasor for bar_sweep; counts as running for 100 ms after
  it last moved. Held at 0 = no sweep.

NOTES
- Steps land at their exact sub-sample time (input interpolated at the tick,
  two-sample polyBLEP on each step); one sample of latency.
- Mid-tread quantizer: silence in = exactly silence out.
- Roughly level-neutral: about -32 dB RMS out with the -32 dB RMS tuba.
- No safety limiter.
