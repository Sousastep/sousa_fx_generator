OVERVIEW
--------
Implements Perttu Hamalainen's (DAFx-2002) order-statistic lookahead peak
limiter. Delays the stereo audio signal by N samples while the sidechain
computes a pre-compensated clipping-control target c(n) (Eq. 14/19), passes it
through a running-maximum filter x_max(n) = max[c(n-N),...,c(n)] (Eq. 6), a
1-pole attack/release detector (Eq. 7), and an M-sample recursive moving-average
filter (Eq. 17/21). Drapes a smooth "cloth" envelope over signal peaks that
prevents clipping with zero click artifacts even under +30 dB of brickwall slam.

PARAMETERS (7 total)
--------------------
1. drive_db        [0.0 .. 30.0 dB, default: 9.0]
   Input loudness maximizer drive before the brickwall ceiling.

2. ceiling_db      [-18.0 .. -0.05 dB, default: -0.3]
   Absolute brickwall peak ceiling threshold in dBFS.

3. lookahead_samp  [4.0 .. 128.0 samples, default: 48.0]
   Feedforward delay N in samples (Eq. 5) and running-max window length (Eq. 6).

4. release_ms      [10.0 .. 1000.0 ms, default: 120.0]
   Envelope release time constant after peaks subside.

5. boxcar_smooth   [1.0 .. 64.0 samples, default: 24.0]
   Length M of the recursive moving-average filter (Eq. 17, 21) smoothing the
   corners of the running-max envelope.

6. pump_character  [0.0 .. 1.0, default: 0.35]
   Sculpts the release contour between ultra-transparent mastering limiting
   (0.0) and rhythmic French-house / drum-bus release breathing (1.0).

7. mix             [0.0 .. 1.0, default: 1.0]
   Dry/wet blend (note: when < 1.0, mixes lookahead-delayed signal with dry).
