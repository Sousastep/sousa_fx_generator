OVERVIEW
--------
Combines local all-pass phase dispersion around transients (which redistributes
spectral phase to smear sharp crest factors by up to 5 dB, Sec. 1) with Fontana
et al.'s (DAFx-2026) exact algebraic clipping-prevention bound on time-varying
all-pass coefficient increments Delta_c(n) (Eqs. 4-7, Algorithm 1). When a sharp
transient drives the envelope-modulated all-pass pole frequency, Algorithm 1
adaptively constrains Delta_c(n) sample-by-sample so the output never exceeds the
prescribed magnitude threshold M, creating a liquid, laser-like phase-dispersed
peak limiter.

PARAMETERS (7 total)
--------------------
1. drive_db        [0.0 .. 20.0 dB, default: 6.0]
   Input gain driving into the hyperbolic pre-ceiling and dispersive all-pass cascade.

2. ceiling_mag     [0.10 .. 1.00, default: 0.92]
   Prescribed linear magnitude ceiling M (Fontana Eq. 4) strictly enforced by
   the coefficient-increment bounds.

3. ap_freq_hz      [60.0 .. 2500.0 Hz, default: 340.0]
   Base break frequency of the first dispersive all-pass stage.

4. env_mod_depth   [0.0 .. 1.0, default: 0.70]
   Depth of transient-envelope modulation sweeping the all-pass pole frequencies
   upward during signal peaks.

5. mod_slew_ms     [0.5 .. 50.0 ms, default: 6.0]
   Release time constant of the transient envelope driving the all-pass coefficients.

6. stage2_spread   [0.0 .. 1.0, default: 0.55]
   Frequency ratio offset of the second cascaded anti-clipping all-pass section
   for broadband transient dispersion.

7. mix             [0.0 .. 1.0, default: 1.0]
   Dry/wet blend (blending < 1.0 creates dynamic phase-cancellation notch limiting).
