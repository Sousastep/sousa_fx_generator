EFFECT: limiter_allpass_phase_antiklip
SOURCE: Fontana, Bernardini, Pasin & D'Angelo (DAFx-2026, "A Clipping Prevention
        Method for All-Pass Digital Filters with Time-Varying Coefficients",
        Sections 1 & 2, Eqs. 1-7, Algorithm 1; citing Parker et al. & Schlecht)
TYPE:   Stereo Dispersive All-Pass Peak Eraser & Algebraically Coefficient-Bounded
        Anti-Clipping Limiter
================================================================================

OVERVIEW
--------
A three-part peak processor, per channel:
1. drive into a tanh soft clipper that keeps the signal inside the ceiling M;
2. two cascaded first-order all-pass filters whose break frequencies jump up
   with the transient envelope (dynamic phase dispersion, Sec. 1);
3. Fontana et al.'s (DAFx-2026) bound on each coefficient change Delta_c(n)
   (Eqs. 4-7, Algorithm 1): when a coefficient move would push the output past
   M, the move is limited (or reversed), so the filter "steers" its phase to
   stay inside the ceiling.
Where the bound cannot hold the output in (a fixed all-pass can itself raise
peaks above M), a final clamp hard-clips at M, so the output never exceeds the
ceiling. The character is soft saturation plus a moving phase "zip" on
attacks, with occasional hard clipping when pushed - not a transparent
limiter. Phase dispersion can also make some peaks higher before the clamp,
so do not expect it to lower crest factor on every signal.

PARAMETERS (7 total)
--------------------
1. drive_db        [0.0 .. 20.0 dB, default: 6.0]
   Input gain into the tanh soft clipper (ceiling M) and the all-pass cascade.
   No makeup: more drive = louder and more saturated, up to the ceiling.

2. ceiling_mag     [0.10 .. 1.00, default: 0.92]
   Linear output ceiling M (Fontana Eq. 4): 1.00 = 0 dBFS, 0.92 = -0.7 dBFS,
   0.5 = -6 dBFS. Enforced by the coefficient bounds and, as a last resort, a
   hard clamp.

3. ap_freq_hz      [60.0 .. 2500.0 Hz, default: 340.0]
   Base break frequency of the first dispersive all-pass stage.

4. env_mod_depth   [0.0 .. 1.0, default: 0.70]
   Depth of transient-envelope modulation sweeping the all-pass break
   frequencies upward during signal peaks (up to 5.5x ap_freq_hz at full depth
   for a 0 dBFS driven signal, more when drive pushes the level higher).

5. mod_slew_ms     [0.5 .. 50.0 ms, default: 6.0]
   Release time constant of the transient envelope driving the all-pass
   coefficients (attack is fixed at 0.8 ms).

6. stage2_spread   [0.0 .. 1.0, default: 0.55]
   Break frequency of the second all-pass relative to the first:
   1.2x (0) .. 4x (1); 0.55 = 2.7x.

7. mix             [0.0 .. 1.0, default: 1.0]
   Dry/wet blend. Below 1.0 the dry and phase-shifted signals partly cancel,
   giving moving notches (a phaser-like colour), and the dry part is not
   ceiling-limited, so the output can exceed M.

INLETS / OUTLETS
----------------
- in1 / in2: stereo audio. If in2 stays silent for 250 ms, in1 is used for both
  sides (so a mono patch into in1 works).
- in3 / in4: not used.
- out1 / out2: stereo. The two channels are processed independently (no link).
