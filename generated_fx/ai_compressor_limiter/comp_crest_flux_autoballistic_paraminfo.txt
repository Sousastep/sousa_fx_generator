OVERVIEW
--------
Implements the signal-adaptive compressor architecture of Maddams et al.
(DAFx-2012, Sec. 4.2) and Hilsamer & Herzog (DAFx-2014). Attack and release
ballistics are continuously modulated in real time by both instantaneous Crest
Factor (Peak-to-RMS ratio in dB) and 2-band Spectral Flux onset velocity,
dropping attack time down toward 40 microseconds (1176 FET speed) on sharp
transients and releasing rapidly to avoid pumping and "breathing" drop-outs.
Also includes Maddams' "Threshold Mode" (Sec. 4.2.1, Fig. 2A) where knee width
scales dynamically with |Threshold| toward an infinite-ratio soft limiter.

PARAMETERS (8 total)
--------------------
1. thresh_db         [-40.0 .. 0.0 dB, default: -20.0]
   Compression threshold (with automatic loudness makeup compensation).

2. ratio             [1.5 .. 30.0, default: 8.0]
   Base compression ratio in "Ratio Mode" (when var_knee_mode = 0).

3. var_knee_mode     [0.0 .. 1.0, default: 0.65]
   Morphs between Maddams' "Ratio Mode" (0.0: fixed 3 dB knee, variable ratio,
   Fig. 2B) and "Threshold Mode" (1.0: ratio -> inf:1 with knee width = |T|,
   Fig. 2A).

4. base_attack_ms    [0.1 .. 50.0 ms, default: 8.0]
   Nominal steady-state attack time before crest/spectral-flux acceleration.

5. base_release_ms   [20.0 .. 800.0 ms, default: 180.0]
   Nominal steady-state release time before crest/spectral-flux acceleration.

6. crest_flux_adapt  [0.0 .. 1.0, default: 0.75]
   Depth of real-time Crest-Factor and Spectral-Flux modulation accelerating
   attack and release times during percussive transients.

7. fet_slam_sat      [0.0 .. 1.0, default: 0.40]
   1176-style FET "All-Buttons-In" harmonic saturation driven by gain reduction.

8. mix               [0.0 .. 1.0, default: 1.0]
   Dry/wet parallel compression blend.
