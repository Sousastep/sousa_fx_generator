EFFECT: comp_crest_flux_autoballistic
SOURCE: Maddams, Finn & Reiss (DAFx-2012, "An Autonomous Method for Multi-Track
        Dynamic Range Compression", Section 4.2, Fig. 2A/2B; citing Giannoulis
        et al.) and Hilsamer & Herzog (DAFx-2014, Sections 2-3, Fig. 1)
TYPE:   Stereo Crest-Factor & Spectral-Flux Adaptive Auto-Ballistic FET/VCA
        Compressor with Variable-Knee Threshold Mode
================================================================================

OVERVIEW
--------
A stereo-linked peak compressor whose attack and release speed up on
transients. Two detectors decide what a transient is: the crest factor
(peak-to-RMS ratio, ~3 dB on a held note, higher on spiky material) and a
2-band spectral-flux onset detector (how fast the low and high band envelopes
are rising relative to their own recent level - level independent, ~0 on a
sustained note). On a hard attack the attack time drops to 12% of
base_attack_ms and the release to 22% of base_release_ms, so tongued notes are
caught quickly and let go quickly, while sustained notes get the slower base
ballistics and do not pump. Also includes Maddams' "Threshold Mode"
(Sec. 4.2.1, Fig. 2A), where the knee widens with |threshold| toward a 40:1
soft limiter.

The level detector is a peak follower with a fixed 1 ms attack, so the overall
attack can never be much faster than ~1 ms even when the gain smoother is set
to its 40 us floor. The adaptive design follows Maddams Sec. 4.2; the
Hilsamer & Herzog reference is background for the crest-factor idea, not a
port of their offline method.

PARAMETERS (8 total)
--------------------
1. thresh_db         [-40.0 .. 0.0 dB, default: -20.0]
   Compression threshold. Automatic makeup gain follows it (about half of the
   reduction a 0 dBFS peak would get), so lowering the threshold squashes
   harder without a big level drop.

2. ratio             [1.5 .. 30.0, default: 8.0]
   Compression ratio in "Ratio Mode" (var_knee_mode = 0). As var_knee_mode
   rises the slope is blended toward 40:1, so the effective ratio is higher
   than this number: at the default 0.65 a ratio of 8 behaves like ~17:1, a
   ratio of 2 like ~5:1.

3. var_knee_mode     [0.0 .. 1.0, default: 0.65]
   Morphs between Maddams' "Ratio Mode" (0.0: fixed 3 dB knee, ratio from the
   ratio knob, Fig. 2B) and "Threshold Mode" (1.0: 40:1 with knee width =
   |threshold|, Fig. 2A). Low thresholds in Threshold Mode give a very wide,
   gentle knee.

4. base_attack_ms    [0.1 .. 50.0 ms, default: 8.0]
   Attack time on sustained material. Transients shorten it (down to 12% at
   full activity, never below 40 us).

5. base_release_ms   [20.0 .. 800.0 ms, default: 180.0]
   Release time on sustained material. Transients shorten it (down to 22% at
   full activity, never below 8 ms).

6. crest_flux_adapt  [0.0 .. 1.0, default: 0.75]
   How much the crest/flux detectors speed up attack and release. 0 = plain
   compressor at the base times.

7. fet_slam_sat      [0.0 .. 1.0, default: 0.40]
   1176-style "All-Buttons-In" saturation driven by gain reduction. Unity gain
   on quiet signals; the clip point falls from 0 dBFS with no gain reduction
   to about -12 dBFS at 20 dB of gain reduction, so it only bites when the
   compressor is working hard. 1 = full tanh clip, 0 = clean.

8. mix               [0.0 .. 1.0, default: 1.0]
   Dry/wet parallel compression blend.

INLETS / OUTLETS
----------------
- in1 / in2: stereo audio. If in2 stays silent for 250 ms, in1 is used for both
  sides (so a mono patch into in1 works).
- in3 / in4: not used.
- out1 / out2: stereo, stereo-linked gain (one gain for both sides).

NOTES
-----
- No latency, no lookahead: very fast peaks can overshoot by a fraction of a
  millisecond. Put limiter_hamalainen_orderstat_peak after it for a hard
  ceiling.
- Makeup can push loud peaks above 0 dBFS when the threshold is low and the
  attack slow; fet_slam_sat or a limiter after it catches that.
