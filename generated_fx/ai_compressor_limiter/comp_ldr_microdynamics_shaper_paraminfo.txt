EFFECT: comp_ldr_microdynamics_shaper
SOURCE: Nercessian, McClellan & Lukin (DAFx-2022, "A Direct Microdynamics
        Adjusting Processor with Matching Paradigm and Differentiable

TYPE:   Stereo Threshold-Free Relative-Level (LDR) Microdynamics Compressor,
        Upward Sustainer & Transient Punch Expander
================================================================================

OVERVIEW
--------
Implements the iZotope Loudness Dynamic Range (LDR) relative-level processor
(DAFx-2022). By defining signal strength as the ratio L[n] = a_short[n] / a_long[n]
between a fast asymmetric ballistic envelope and a slow macrodynamic envelope,
the processor has NO threshold dead-zone and is invariant to input gain or
verse/chorus macrodynamic changes (down to about -50 dBFS RMS; below that the
processing fades out by -70 dBFS so silence and noise are not pumped up).
Applying g[n] = L[n]^((m-1)/p) (Eq. 12) directly compresses microdynamics and pulls up room/sustain when m < 1.0, or
expands transient punch and articulation when m > 1.0.

PARAMETERS (8 total)
--------------------
1. micro_amount    [0.0 .. 3.5, default: 0.35]
   Direct microdynamics scaling exponent m (Eq. 11-12):
   - 0.0 .. 0.99: Microdynamic compression & upward sustain inflation (0.0 = flat sustain).
   - 1.0:         Unity bypass (exact below -3 dBFS output).
   - 1.01 .. 3.5: Microdynamic expansion & snappy transient punch enhancement.

2. attack_ms       [0.2 .. 80.0 ms, default: 5.0]
   Short-term envelope rise time constant tau_up (Eq. 9).

3. release_ms      [5.0 .. 500.0 ms, default: 85.0]
   Short-term envelope fall time constant tau_down (Eq. 9).

4. macro_scale     [2.0 .. 40.0, default: 12.0]
   Long-term macrodynamic window multiplier s (tau_long = s * max(tau_up, tau_down),
   Eq. 10) separating microdynamics from phrase-level macrodynamics.

5. max_boost_db    [15.0 .. 42.0 dB, default: 24.0]
   Maximum gain change in dB, both ways: caps the upward sustain boost and the
   cut, so it limits how far a tail is pulled up (or a transient pushed) at
   extreme micro_amount settings.

6. stereo_link     [0.0 .. 1.0, default: 0.75]
   Blends between dual-mono relative-level detection (0.0) and stereo-linked
   energy detection (1.0).

7. trim_db         [-12.0 .. 12.0 dB, default: 0.0]
   Manual output trim on top of the automatic inverse-linked makeup gain c
   (c = 2.2 dB x (1 - micro_amount), clamped to -6..+4 dB; +1.4 dB at the
   default).

8. mix             [0.0 .. 1.0, default: 1.0]
   Dry/wet parallel microdynamics blend.

INLETS / OUTLETS
----------------
- in1 / in2: stereo audio. If in2 stays silent for 250 ms, in1 is used for both
  sides (so a mono patch into in1 works).
- in3 / in4: not used.
- out1 / out2: stereo.

NOTES
-----
- The wet signal passes a soft ceiling: unity below -3 dBFS, a smooth knee
  above, never exceeding 0 dBFS. It only engages when expansion or trim pushes
  the output that hot.
