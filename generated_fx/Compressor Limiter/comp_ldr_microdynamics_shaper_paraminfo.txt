OVERVIEW
--------
Implements the iZotope Loudness Dynamic Range (LDR) relative-level processor
(DAFx-2022). By defining signal strength as the ratio L[n] = a_short[n] / a_long[n]
between a fast asymmetric ballistic envelope and a slow macrodynamic envelope,
the processor has NO threshold dead-zone and is completely invariant to input
gain or verse/chorus macrodynamic changes. Applying g[n] = L[n]^((m-1)/p) (Eq. 12)
directly compresses microdynamics and pulls up room/sustain when m < 1.0, or
expands transient punch and articulation when m > 1.0.

PARAMETERS (8 total)
--------------------
1. micro_amount    [0.0 .. 3.5, default: 0.35]
   Direct microdynamics scaling exponent m (Eq. 11-12):
   - 0.0 .. 0.99: Microdynamic compression & upward sustain inflation (0.0 = flat sustain).
   - 1.0:         Unity bypass.
   - 1.01 .. 3.5: Microdynamic expansion & snappy transient punch enhancement.

2. attack_ms       [0.2 .. 80.0 ms, default: 5.0]
   Short-term envelope rise time constant tau_up (Eq. 9).

3. release_ms      [5.0 .. 500.0 ms, default: 85.0]
   Short-term envelope fall time constant tau_down (Eq. 9).

4. macro_scale     [2.0 .. 40.0, default: 12.0]
   Long-term macrodynamic window multiplier s (tau_long = s * max(tau_up, tau_down),
   Eq. 10) separating microdynamics from phrase-level macrodynamics.

5. max_boost_db    [15.0 .. 42.0 dB, default: 24.0]
   Maximum upward sustain boost / transient expansion range in dB.

6. stereo_link     [0.0 .. 1.0, default: 0.75]
   Blends between dual-mono relative-level detection (0.0) and stereo-linked
   energy detection (1.0).

7. trim_db         [-12.0 .. 12.0 dB, default: 0.0]
   Manual output trim on top of the automatic inverse-linked makeup gain c.

8. mix             [0.0 .. 1.0, default: 1.0]
   Dry/wet parallel microdynamics blend.
