================================================================================
hilbert_envelope — Parameter Information
================================================================================

WHAT IT IS
The amplitude of the analytic signal: a quadrature allpass pair makes the real
and 90-degree-shifted copies of the tuba, and sqrt(re^2 + im^2) is the
instantaneous amplitude. There is no rectifier, no threshold and no release —
the reading follows the waveform's own amplitude, so it is the fastest and most
transient-accurate envelope in the batch. A short one-pole after the magnitude
smooths the reading.

Important: this is the exact envelope of a narrowband signal, not a loudness
meter. A tuba is a harmonic stack, so |analytic| also contains the waveform's
intra-cycle shape; on a 30-60 Hz note it will visibly follow that shape. That
is the algorithm working as defined, not a bug. Raise smooth_ms (30-50 ms) for
a loudness-like contour, or use peak_ar_follower / rms_true_follower /
cascade_env_follower when you want a smooth loudness envelope.

Source: Dutilleux, DAFx-98, §5.1 (instantaneous envelope detector as the
magnitude of the analytic signal), §5.4 (no averager = finest resolution).

PARAMETERS (2)

1. smooth_ms — 0.5…50 ms, default 10 ms
   Smoothing after the analytic magnitude. For a pure sine, 10 ms leaves about
   3.2% ripple at 30 Hz and under 0.1% above 55 Hz. On a real tuba the harmonic
   stack dominates the low-note ripple: 10 ms still shows the waveform shape at
   30 Hz, 30-50 ms gives a loudness-like contour, and 2-5 ms keeps the most
   transient detail at the cost of a visibly waveform-shaped envelope.

2. out_db — -24…+24 dB, default 0 dB
   Level of the control signal on out1. The analytic amplitude of a sine is its
   peak amplitude, not its RMS.

INLETS / OUTLETS
- in1: tuba audio (mono).
- out1: analytic amplitude, 0..1 before out_db, at audio rate.

NOTES
- The allpass recursion is y = c*(x + y1) - x1, H(z) = (c - z^-1)/(1 - c z^-1).
  That is the pole-at-c form the cR/cI coefficients were fitted for; the other
  allpass sign convention is not in quadrature with them.
- The coefficients assume 44.1/48 kHz. At 96 kHz the quadrature drifts at the
  bottom of the tuba range, so run this at 44.1 or 48 kHz.
- The output is a control signal, not a monitor signal.
- No safety limiter, no metering, no gate.
