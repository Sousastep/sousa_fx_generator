================================================================================
cascade_env_follower — Parameter Information
================================================================================

WHAT IT IS
A peak envelope smoothed by several attack/release one-poles in series instead
of one. Each stage starts from the previous stage's output, so the envelope's
slope is continuous at the attack-to-release switch: the control signal sounds
smooth rather than steppy when it drives gain, cutoff or pitch. The stage
coefficients are normalized by the composite step time, so attack_ms and
release_ms mean the same thing they do on the single-stage follower even when
you change the order.

Source: Sanfilippo, SMC-22, §2.2 Eqs. 2-4 (cascaded exponential smoothers),
§2.4 (overshoot/THD).

PARAMETERS (4)

1. attack_ms — 0.1…100 ms, default 5 ms
   Composite 63% attack time of the whole cascade. Individual stages run faster
   by q_M (4.35 at 4 stages, 8.64 at 8 stages) so the sum still lands here.

2. release_ms — 5…2000 ms, default 300 ms
   Composite 63% release time. Long values give a sustained body; short values
   let the envelope breathe with the valve rhythm.

3. stages — 2…8, default 4
   Cascade order. 2 is close to a plain AR follower; 8 is the smoothest and
   costs the most (one extra one-pole per stage per sample). 4 is the useful
   middle for audio-rate modulation.

4. out_db — -24…+24 dB, default 0 dB
   Level of the control signal on out1. 0 dB leaves the natural 0..1 range.

INLETS / OUTLETS
- in1: tuba audio (mono).
- out1: envelope control signal, 0..1 linear before out_db, at audio rate.

NOTES
- More stages soften the attack corner and add a little group delay, but the
  63% timing stays at attack_ms/release_ms by construction.
- The paper's C_M cutoff normalization is replaced here by a step-time
  normalization q_M, because this effect is a control-signal follower where
  timing, not the -3 dB corner, is what the knobs promise.
- The output is a control signal, not a monitor signal.
- No safety limiter, no metering, no gate.
