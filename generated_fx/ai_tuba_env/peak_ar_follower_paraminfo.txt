================================================================================
peak_ar_follower — Parameter Information
================================================================================

WHAT IT IS
A full-wave peak follower with separate attack and release times: the plainest
way to turn the tuba's instantaneous amplitude into a control signal. It reacts
to every rectified sample, so it is the most articulate and the most "literal"
of the batch — good for transient-shaped modulation (filter blips, envelope
accents), less good for a smooth loudness reading.

Source: Floru, AES 104th Convention 1998, preprint 4703, §1-2 (peak detector);
Dutilleux, DAFx-98, §5.1-5.2 (full-wave detector + AR-averager).

PARAMETERS (3)

1. attack_ms — 0.1…100 ms, default 5 ms
   Time for the envelope to reach 63% of a rising step. Short values (0.5-2 ms)
   keep the valve tongue and the lip transient; long values soften the onset.

2. release_ms — 5…2000 ms, default 250 ms
   Time for the envelope to fall 63% after the input drops. Short is punchy and
   lets the follower chatter on a decaying note; long holds the body through
   gaps between notes.

3. out_db — -24…+24 dB, default 0 dB
   Level of the control signal on out1. 0 dB keeps the envelope in its natural
   0..1 range; raise it only when feeding something that expects a hotter signal.

INLETS / OUTLETS
- in1: tuba audio (mono).
- out1: envelope control signal, 0..1 linear before out_db, at audio rate.

NOTES
- The output is a control signal, not a monitor signal. Patch it into a
  modulation input, or listen to it through a VCA.
- No safety limiter, no metering, no gate.
